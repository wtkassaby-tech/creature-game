# Concept sheet review (Meshy, 24 November 2026)

One Meshy concept sheet for every creature in scaffolds A to F, plus two creatures Ayrton asked for during the run (Deltail and Mintcrag). Each sheet is a 16:9 multi-view (front, side, back) saved as a 1280x714 WebP under `art/meshy/concepts/<Biome>/<Name>.webp`. Images only: no 3D models were made.

## How the run was set up

- **Settings:** Nano Banana Pro, Multi-view on, 1 image, 9 credits per sheet. Before every Generate a script checked that the one Multi-view switch was on, the cost shown was 9 and the prompt length matched the intended prompt.
- **Skipped (already done):** Mossmitt, Spadesnout (Biped; its Quad variant also skipped), Sloshcalf and The Firstling. Pebblepot was also skipped: the scaffold marks it "only if Spadesnout stays a Quad", and the Biped was chosen, so Loamlet is the ninth Greenwood creature.
- **Eye rule (Ayrton, during the run):** after Gorsling's first sheet came back with small dot eyes and a little "o" mouth ("no personality in its eyes"), every remaining prompt got one short expression phrase taken from that creature's personality line (for example "big glossy eyes in a cocky smirk, one brow raised" for Duneskit, or "big glossy eyes rolled up in proud off-key rapture" for Forkhum). To stay within 800 characters, the now-redundant "Three-quarter front view" was dropped (multi-view sets the views), and "only eyes, glass and water shine" became "only the eyes shine" where a creature has no glass or water parts. Small, dotty or vacant eyes were treated as a defect worth a regeneration.
- **Reference for judging (Ayrton):** Stiltbark, the owner favourite. One clear, specific emotion in big eyes, an awkward or funny body that fits the personality, and a slightly odd silhouette; not generic-cute. Every verdict below is judged against that and the eye rule, plus the earlier failure list (chest bumps, a face in a hood, small googly eyes, text, wrong body plan, clothing or props, glossy plastic, anything creepy).
- **Burlhorn and Stiltbark** were made before the eye rule. On re-check both have clear, expressive eyes, so they were kept and their prompts are unchanged.
- **Scaffold files:** every prompt actually used now sits in its creature's "Short prompt" slot with its character count and a dated note; the original is kept just below it as "Previous version (not used):". Where a creature was regenerated, the slot holds the prompt of the sheet that was kept.

## Totals

- 116 sheets kept: 114 scaffold creatures and 2 extras. Verdicts: 63 strong, 51 good, 2 weak.
- 141 generations at 9 credits = 1,269 credits. Balance went from 4,685 to 3,416.
  - 116 first sheets, 24 regenerations for defects (listed in the tables), and 1 misfire: the first Stumpwarden task picked up the Stiltbark prompt because the second prompt was filled in before the first Generate had been sent. After that, each Generate was followed by a wait, and every batch was checked against Meshy's task list.
  - Three Generate clicks did not register while someone was also using the page (Capgnome, Bolide, Galaxeel). They cost nothing and were sent again.
- Rejected first versions are kept beside the final files as `<Name>_v1.webp` (or `_v2` where the earlier version was kept), so Ayrton can compare.

- **Puffchin:** clean and happy but generic. "Scatterbrained" never comes through, and the finish is smooth rather than clay.
