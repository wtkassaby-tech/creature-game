# ClayPals: Roblox store page copy

Draft for Ayrton. Working title ClayPals. Roblox limits: title 50 characters, description 1000 characters. Comparable pages reviewed (Adopt Me, Creatures of Sonaria, Pet Simulator): all open with a plain verb pitch (collect, raise, trade, grow), then a short feature list. Nothing here copies their wording.

## Title
ClayPals: Tame, Breed, Battle [Story]

Alternates: ClayPals | Creature Tamers; ClayPals: Breed Your Own Creatures

## First-line hook (the line before the "read more" cut)
Tame handmade clay creatures, breed new ones from their parts, and chase the legend at the end of the map.

## Description (about 900 characters, paste as is)
Welcome to a world sculpted from clay! Throw your lasso, tame creatures, and build a team of six.

Every creature is made of parts. Breed two and the baby wears pieces of both parents: head, body, legs, tail. Raise families across generations and make something nobody else has.

TAME: time your lasso throw to win a creature over. No tricks, just skill.
BATTLE: turn-based fights with elemental types. Smart teams beat big numbers.
BREED: part-based, generational. Every egg is a surprise you can read.
EXPLORE: eleven biomes, from Greenwood to the Hollow, each with its own towns, tamers and a giant.
STORY: a rival, a mentor, and a rumour about a tear in the sky.
TRADE: swap creatures with friends. Rare ones carry serial numbers.
Beat each giant, win the badge, open the next biome, and find the ultimate mythic creature.

Free to play. Please report bugs and ideas in the group!

## Feature bullets (for the page, trailer card or Discord)
- Lasso taming: a timing minigame, judged by the server, no pay-to-win catches.
- Part-based breeding: offspring visibly mix both parents. Family trees show the lineage.
- Turn-based battles with ten elemental types and teams that grow from 3 to 6.
- Eleven biomes with towns, routes, tamers, giants and gate battles (first three at launch).
- Giants: huge scripted bosses with phases, and one hard capture attempt after each win.
- A story with a rival and a mentor; every chapter teaches a mechanic.
- Rare creatures with serial numbers, tradeable between players.
- Hidden caches, daily gathering and journal sets to reward exploring.
- Cozy handmade clay-toy look, built for phones first.
- Coins are earned, never sold. Lassos, bait and breeding cost earned coins only.

## Age rating notes (questionnaire guidance)
- Plan: soft launch to ages 16 and over (brief section 11, open item), decide on all ages from retention data.
- Violence: cartoon creature battles, turn-based, no blood, no realistic harm. Creatures "faint". Expect Mild.
- Gambling-like: none. Coin bets and staked matches were removed. Creatures never change hands by battle results.
- Paid random items: if any Robux purchase has random outcomes, show every outcome and odds (summing to 100%) before purchase, and honour `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` and `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed`. Current plan sells no random items.
- Social: trading between players, so answer the questionnaire for user-to-user trading and chat as applicable.
- Fear and crude humour: none. Mild fantasy peril in the story.
- Re-check live Roblox questionnaire and policies before submitting; they changed several times in 2026.
