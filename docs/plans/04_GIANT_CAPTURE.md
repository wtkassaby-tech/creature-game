# Approval pack 4: capturing a giant

Date: 21 November 2026. For: Ayrton. Status: **waiting for your answers**. Giant fights are built and tested; the capture is not, because the brief does not say what a captured giant *is* once it is yours.

## 1. What is already decided

- Beating a giant gives one hard lasso attempt (three loops, narrow zones). Miss, and you fight again with a new Giant Horn. (Brief, locked.)
- Each player can capture each giant once, ever. (Brief, locked.)
- A captured giant gets a serial number in the order players captured it, by the four safe steps you approved in pack 2. A number can be skipped but never given twice.
- Giants are bound to the account for now (pack 2). Whether they can ever be traded or bred is still open in the brief.

## 2. The question: what is a giant on your team?

Hollowbough in its lair is a level 16 boss with about 2.5 times a whole team's health. That cannot join a team as it is. Three ways to do it:

| Option | What the player gets | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. A trophy creature (my recommendation)** | A normal-sized creature version of the giant: its own species with its own five parts, stats at the top of its biome's range (about a gem-tier creature), two signature moves from the fight. It fights like any other creature and obeys the level cap. It cannot breed, so its parts stay unique | A real prize you use every day; simple to balance (it goes through the same balance check as every species); keeps giants special | Needs one extra creature model per giant (five parts in the giant's look) |
| B. A trophy only | A statue for the player's stable and a title, with the serial number on it. Not a fighter | No balance risk, no extra battle art | The brief calls giant capture "the prestige route" to getting a creature; a statue is not a creature |
| C. The full giant as a fighter | The giant itself, huge, with boss stats scaled down | Most spectacular | Breaks PvP and makes every later fight trivial unless heavily scaled, at which point it is option A with more art |

With option A, I would build each giant's creature form as data alongside its fight (species file, parts, moves), run it through the roster balance check, and then build the capture: the lasso after a win, the serial number steps, and the tests for both.

## 3. Smaller questions

1. **Size on screen.** Should a captured giant look bigger than other creatures (say 1.3 times) while fighting like a normal one? I recommend yes: it costs nothing in balance and shows it off.
2. **Can a captured giant be traded?** The brief suggests yes later, "since low serials make great trade items". It is bound for now; opening it is a switch, not a rebuild.
3. **A missed capture.** Today's plan: the giant is beaten, you get the first-clear or rematch rewards either way, and only the capture needs another fight. Confirm that a miss does not take the win's rewards away.
