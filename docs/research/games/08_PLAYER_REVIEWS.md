# What players say about creature-collecting games

Date: 23 November 2026. Status: research, nothing decided. All our content is original; this file is about what players feel, not what to copy.

## How far to trust this

- **Sourced (read)** means I opened the page and read it. **Sourced (summary)** means I only saw a search-result summary of the page, not the page itself. **Recall** means my own background knowledge, not checked.
- Reddit, Bulbagarden, PokeCommunity and the Brick Bronze fan wiki all refused automated fetches (403, 402 or a block). So I have almost no raw player forum posts. Most of what follows is review sites, press pieces about player reaction, and automated review-summary pages (VaporLens). Those summaries are machine-written from Steam reviews. Treat them as direction, not counts.
- "Ranked by how often it comes up" means: how many of the games I checked had a source that raised it. The sample is small. A rank of 1 versus 2 is not a real difference.
- I found no "why I quit" YouTube video for any of the Roblox games. I could not check video titles at all.
- Quotes are kept to one, and short. Everything else is paraphrased.

## Summary (half a page)

1. **Players love creatures they find charming, a catch that feels earned, and doing it with friends.** Designs and animation are praised for nearly every game on the list (Cassette Beasts, Ooblets, Palworld, Nexomon, Monster Sanctuary, Temtem). Music comes next. Co-op and trading are the main reason people stay (Temtem, Palworld, Brick Bronze, Adopt Me).
2. **Grind is the single biggest complaint, and it is everywhere.** Seven of the games checked have grind named as the main fault. It is mostly levelling, farming a rare creature, or fighting the same battle again and again.
3. **Slow battles and slow walking come second.** Cassette Beasts players ask for a speed-up. Coromon and Monster Sanctuary boss fights are called drawn-out. Reviewers praise short, snappy formats.
4. **Bugs, crashes and abandoned games lose players fast.** Scarlet and Violet, Temtem, Cassette Beasts and Palworld all drew complaints about crashes. Temtem and Cassette Beasts are accused of going quiet after launch.
5. **Paying for power is resented; paying for random results is a child-safety issue.** Temtem's battle pass drew fire. Adopt Me is flagged by parent groups and a university study for scams and random-reward spending. This matches our rules.
6. **Brick Bronze and Project Pokemon are missed for three reasons:** it was free on every device, you could see and fight other real players in the same towns and grass, and it felt like a full, faithful adventure. Sadness about the takedown comes up more than any single feature.
7. **Children and parents** call these games fun, colourful and a good first step. Their worries are scams, bullying and spending. Kids found the easy games easy and some found pacing and confusing parts frustrating.
8. **For us:** keep the first hour fast, make battles skippable and short, protect trading from scams, give every system a plain on-screen explanation, ship regular small updates, and do not let any catch or breed depend on spending. Details below.

## What children and younger players say

Kept apart from adults. The evidence is thin: Common Sense Media pages for kids are AI-written summaries of a few dozen reviews, and I could not read individual kid reviews.

- **Adopt Me (kid reviews, summary of about 22):** kids like fun gameplay, learning to look after pets, being creative and socialising. They dislike inappropriate content, bullying and scams. Sourced (read): [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/website-reviews/adopt-me/user-reviews/child). The adult rating is 13+, citing time investment and contact with strangers ([search summary](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/website-reviews/adopt-me)).
- **Adopt Me (Parent Zone):** children like decorating houses, raising pets, earning rare ones, and a game that keeps changing with suggestions from players. Parents worry about scams, trades outside the game, big bills and the time rare pets take. Sourced (read): [Parent Zone](https://parentzone.org.uk/article/adopt-me).
- **Let's Go (kid reviews, summary of about 21):** kids like it as a first step into Pokemon, with bright visuals and easy play. Some found it too easy, some found pacing frustrating, a few things confusing. Sourced (read): [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/game-reviews/pokemon-lets-go-eeveepikachu/user-reviews/child). Experienced fans scored it far lower than critics did (Metacritic user score about 6 against critics near 80; [search summary](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/game-reviews/pokemon-lets-go-eeveepikachu)).
- **Scarlet/Violet (kid reviews):** kids praise the characters and say the game is challenging. A parent-focused site says older kids (10 to 12) rush the story and want raids and trading. Sourced (summary): [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/game-reviews/pokemon-scarletviolet), [ScreenWise](https://screenwiseapp.com/guides/pokemon-scarlet-violet).
- **Other virtual-pet sites:** kids on Foo Pets said it turned money-driven. Neopets kids reported mixed experiences and bullying. Sourced (summary): [Common Sense Media, via search](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/website-reviews/foopets).
- **Random paid rewards:** researchers, not kids, raise this. See complaint 12.
- Could not find: any direct kid or teen posts about Loomian Legacy, Doodle World, Tanorio, or Catch And Tame.

## What this means for our game

Ranked by how many sources point at it and how cheap it is to do. "Needs approval" means it touches saving, purchases, captures, breeding or trading, so the owner must approve it before we build (CLAUDE.md workflow, step 2).

| # | Action | Why (source type) | Effort | Touches (needs approval?) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
