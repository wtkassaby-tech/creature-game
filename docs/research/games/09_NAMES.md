# 09 Names: the creature word and the game title

Written 23 November 2026. Names are still [Open] in `docs/DECISIONS.md` ("Names: the game, ..."), so this is a proposal for Ayrton, not a decision.

**This is not a legal trademark clearance.** The checks below are ordinary web searches (US-based search tool, so UK, Australian and Roblox-internal results are thin). They find obvious clashes only.

## What the name has to do

"Pokemon Brick Bronze" is a creature word plus a place or flavour word. So we need one word for the creatures (singular and plural, like "a Patchling") and a subtitle. Our game's hooks are: parts bred together (the patchwork), the rope lasso, the homely storybook feel, Thistledown and Greenwood.

## Step 1: the long list

**Creature words considered (the 4 already on the table, plus 20 new):**
Patchlings, Tamelings, Croftlings, Burrowkin, Quiltlings, Stitchlings, Mendlings, Tuftlings, Thistlings, Hobblings, Scrapkins, Cobblings, Tatterlings, Mottlings, Hearthlings, Kindlings, Moppets, Dapplings, Pelties, Bramblings, Lassolings, Critterkin, Jumblings, Snugglings, Nibblings, Rovelings, Hodgekin, Wuzzles.

Cut straight away:
- Wildlings (a famous TV term), Kindlings (a fire-lighting word, and a Pokemon-style fire creature feel), Moppets (an old-fashioned word for a small child), Scrapkins and Tatterlings (sound scruffy or sad), Hodgekin (hard to spell), Wuzzles (an old cartoon and toy line), Jumblings (hard to say), Nibblings (sounds like food), Snugglings (too babyish for 9 to 16), Lassolings (clumsy), Critterkin (generic, hard to search), Rovelings (hard to spell), Hobblings (suggests limping), Cobblings (sounds like cobbled, and "cobbler"), Hearthlings (long and fussy), Dapplings (a pattern word, but weaker than patch), Pelties (close to "pelt" and "peltier", meh), Bramblings (a real bird, the brambling, so searches are swamped), Thistlings (too close to the town name Thistledown, which would blur the two).

**Subtitles considered (the 2 already on the table, plus 15 new):**
Thistledown, Lasso & Hearth, Greenwood, Greenwood Tales, Rope & Ramble, The Patchwork Isles, The Eleven Lands, Wild & Woven, Hearth & Hedgerow, Lasso Trails, The Long Road Home, Lantern Trail, Tales of Thistledown, Heartwood, Meadowmend, Hollow & Hearth, Rope & Ribbon.

Note on the "-lings" ending: Pinata Smashlings (a Roblox creature-capture game with toys) uses it, and there are many others (Hatchlings, for instance, is a filed US game mark). The ending is common and not ownable by anyone, but it means the *whole* name needs to be distinctive, which "Patchlings" and "Quiltlings" are.

## Recommendation

**Creature word: Patchlings.** It is the clearest description of the breeding hook ("made of patches of their parents"), it is the easiest to say and spell, it sounds homely, and the exact word is not used by any creature game, toy or Roblox experience we could find. The two things to know: a tiny itch.io puzzle game uses the same word, and *Patch Quest* is a lasso-taming monster game that is close in idea, so a player might think of it. Neither is the same name, and neither is on Roblox as far as we could see. If Ayrton wants zero risk of that confusion, switch to the first runner-up.

**Runner-ups:**
1. **Quiltlings.** Same "made of parts" meaning, even cosier, and nothing found at all. Slightly longer, and quilts can feel a little gentle for older players, but it fits the storybook tone well.
2. **Mendlings.** Clear, easy and homely, but the meaning (repairing) is a half-step away from breeding, so it explains the game less.

## Titles

Roblox games in this style use creature word, then a place or flavour word. A colon is fine in a game title; many just run the words together ("Patchlings Greenwood").

**For Patchlings (top pick):**
1. **Patchlings: Thistledown.** Uses the home town as the place word, matching the "Brick Bronze" pattern most closely. Short and easy to remember.
2. **Patchlings: Lasso & Hearth.** Names both the rope (the taming hook) and the home feel in four words. Best at telling a stranger what the game is.
3. **Patchlings: Greenwood Tales.** Names the first biome and promises a story; easy to extend later ("Patchlings: Dust Canyon" and so on for updates).

**For Quiltlings (first runner-up):**
1. **Quiltlings: Thistledown.** Same pattern as above, the warmest of the set.
2. **Quiltlings: Lasso & Hearth.** The "quilt" and "hearth" pair reads as a single cosy picture.
3. **Quiltlings: The Eleven Lands.** Points at the eleven biomes and the scale of the game, which the others do not.

## Subtitles: the best 8 (from the 17)

Thistledown, Lasso & Hearth, Greenwood Tales, The Eleven Lands, Hearth & Hedgerow, Rope & Ramble, Lantern Trail, Wild & Woven.

Dropped: Greenwood (too plain next to Thistledown), The Patchwork Isles (repeats the creature word's meaning, and we have lands, not isles), The Long Road Home (sad), Meadowmend and Heartwood (invented words with no search value), Hollow & Hearth (clashes with The Hollow, a late biome), Lasso Trails, Tales of Thistledown and Rope & Ribbon (weaker than the survivors).

## What a proper trademark check would still need

- Searches of the official registers by an IP professional: UK IPO, USPTO, IP Australia and WIPO Global Brand Database, for the exact word and close variants (Patchling, Patchlings, Patchlings in toy, game, app and entertainment classes 9, 28 and 41), including phonetic and look-alike matches.
- A check inside Roblox itself: the Discover search and the Creator Store for the same name, which this web search cannot reach reliably.
- A check of app stores (Apple, Google, Steam, itch.io) and social media handles and domains, since the name needs a home to be found.
- A deliberate look at *Patch Quest* (Curve Games) and any owner of "Patch" or "-lings" marks in games and toys, to judge whether the names are confusingly similar.
- A decision on whether to register the name, and in which countries, with a solicitor or trademark attorney.
