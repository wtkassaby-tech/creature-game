# Pokemon screens, looked at directly

22 November 2026. The research agents cannot see pictures, so these seven screens were opened and looked at one by one. Each note says what is on the screen and what we could take from it. We take the pattern only: no creature, icon, font or layout is copied.

Source: screenshots hosted on Bulbapedia's archive (`archives.bulbagarden.net`), linked from its "Pokémon battle" and "Party" pages.

## What the seven have in common

1. **The creature is always the biggest, brightest thing on the screen.** Menus sit at the edges.
2. **Rounded cards, not plain lists.** One creature, one card: portrait, name, level, health bar.
3. **Colour carries meaning.** Type colours on moves, green to red on health, one highlight colour for "selected", blue against red for the two sides.
4. **The world stays visible** behind menus (blurred or dimmed), so a menu feels like a pause, not a different place.
5. **Short labels with an icon each.** Almost no sentences on any screen.
6. **Button hints along the bottom edge.** On a phone that space belongs to the thumbstick and jump button, so our hints must go elsewhere.
