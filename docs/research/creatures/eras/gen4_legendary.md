# Gen 4 (Sinnoh, HGSS): legendaries, mythicals, mascots

Scope: Diamond/Pearl/Platinum/HeartGold/SoulSilver. What made the big creatures feel special, what landed, what did not. Principles only; we copy no creature.

## Evidence gathered
- Official 2020 Pokemon of the Year vote (Google + The Pokemon Company, 6.6M+ votes): Lucario led Sinnoh with 102,259 votes (2nd overall), Garchomp second with 61,877 (7th overall). Luxray, Piplup, Infernape followed. Legendaries sat lower: a user-posted Q&A (unofficial, unconfirmed) has Darkrai 6th and Giratina 10th in Sinnoh. Only the top 30 per region was published. Sources: Bulbapedia "Pokemon of the Year"; Nintendo Wire; Pokemon Database news.
- Takeaway: the beloved Sinnoh faces are the *non-legendary* mascots, a bipedal fighter and a land-shark dragon. Legendaries are admired but rank below.
- Japanese polls: Arceus ranked first in the 2010 poll aimed at dedicated players (Engadget/Siliconera); Arceus and Mew were 4th/5th in a 2016 top-100 (GameSpot). Lugia was 2nd and Suicune 5th in a 2016 "most handsome" goo poll, Darkrai 9th (Anime News Network, Twinfinite). Bulbapedia data lists Lugia 3rd (53,268) and Suicune 9th (26,227) in an earlier fan vote; I could not verify that poll's identity.
- Small poll (Bulbagarden, 66 voters, five options only): Giratina 22 votes (33.3%), Palkia 8 (12.1%). Too small to rely on.
- Sales: DP about 17.6M, HGSS 12.72M (Nintendo IR via Siliconera). Remakes sold strongly on legendary-box-art nostalgia, though no source in my search isolates the legendary effect.
- Design commentary (Game Developer/Gamasutra "Evolution of Pokemon Design: Gen 4"): 14 legendary/mythical creatures, the most of any generation. Praised: abstract concept creatures (Heatran as lava monster, Darkrai as nightmare); Dialga/Palkia/Giratina echo Arceus's face, so the family reads as made by one creator. Criticised: Uxie/Mesprit/Azelf as the first "copy-paste" design, sharing a look with each other and with Mew.
- TheGamer's ranking: Heatran 13th (too unlike a typical legendary, no matching ability), Dialga 12th (time theme overlaps Celebi), Mesprit 11th (even stats, no flavour), Giratina 6th, Regigigas 5th, Palkia 4th (a more distinct twist on its theme than Dialga). Uxie praised for stats matching theme (knowledge: high defence, tricky moves); Azelf's offence and speed fit willpower.
- Giratina: Platinum's Origin Forme made it the "opposite" of Dialga/Palkia (Wikipedia mirror, original unverified); serpentine, with ghost/dragon/centipede (omukade yokai) influences; the Distortion World gave it a place to live. Critical reception strong, though IGN called it "kind of terrible to look at".
- Darkrai: fans cite gothic silhouette, smoke-like form, and a misunderstood/tragic reading (no one wants to hurt anyone, it just gives nightmares). Listicle sources; treat as fan interpretation.
- Regigigas: repeatedly named a weak design (goofy yellow ridges on black/white body; Slow Start ability) despite the "created the Regis" lore (Ranker, TechRaptor).
- Fan complaint that legendaries became less interesting after Gen 4 and that there were "too many" (PokeCommunity threads), plus Psychic-type overload.

## Cautions
Fan lists are opinion; several sources are content farms. No primary Sugimori/Game Freak statement on these designs was found.

## Lessons for ClayPals
1. Legendaries need a visual family: shared motif (face, colour, material) across one myth cluster makes a set feel authored. Our "elder" creatures can share one clay glaze or sculpting mark.
2. Mascots win love, legendaries win awe. Spend charm budget (readable silhouette, expressive face) on common-to-mid creatures; give the rare ones strangeness.
3. Copy-paste sets disappoint. A trio needs distinct silhouettes, not palette swaps; differentiate by shape first, colour second.
4. Make the stats, ability and theme say the same thing (Uxie/Azelf). Theme overlap (Dialga vs Celebi) reads as redundant: one concept per slot.
5. A place and a state change make a creature memorable (Giratina's second form and its own world). For us: a rare variant that appears only in a particular biome or breed combination.
6. Sympathy plus menace (Darkrai) outlasts pure menace. Give dark creatures a gentle reason.
7. Avoid goofy pattern on a huge body (Regigigas): scale needs a clear, simple big shape and few details, which suits chunky clay anyway.
8. Do not mass-produce rares: 14 in one generation diluted the specialness.
