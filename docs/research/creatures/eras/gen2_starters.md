# Generation 2 (Gold/Silver/Crystal): starters and early-route commons

Scope: Johto starters and lines, babies, early-route commons. Design principles only; nothing here is to be copied.

## Evidence (with numbers)

- **Pokemon of the Year 2020 (official fan vote, via Anime News Network).** Overall: Umbreon 5th, Tyranitar 12th, Lugia 15th. Gen 2 regional top 10: Umbreon, Tyranitar, Lugia, Typhlosion (4th), Scizor, Ampharos, Espeon, Cyndaquil (8th), Suicune, Totodile (10th). Chikorita and Meganium are not in the Gen 2 top 10. No common route creature appears in the top 10.
- **Bulbapedia's vote table** (a search summary reported Cyndaquil 28,332 votes, Totodile 22,526, Chikorita 18,521, Umbreon 67,062). I could not open the page (403), so treat those counts as unverified.
- **Nintendo Life Gen 2 reader poll, 4,133 votes.** Umbreon 8%, Tyranitar 6%, Lugia 6%, Totodile 5%, Cyndaquil/Typhlosion/Ampharos/Scizor 4% each, Espeon/Heracross 3%. At 2%: Chikorita, Feraligatr, Crobat, Togepi, Pichu, Wooper. Marill, Meganium and Quagsire 0.9%. Informal, but it shows the commons that punch above their weight: Togepi, Pichu, Wooper.
- **Goo Ranking "cutest starter" (Japan, 2,613 votes, via Kotaku).** Chikorita 4th (231), Cyndaquil 7th (149), Totodile tied 16th (33). So Chikorita wins on cute while Cyndaquil and Totodile win on cool/power.
- **Chikorita as "useless"** (Crunchyroll Twitter poll, May 2018, about 1,400 respondents): voted most useless starter. Cute appeal does not equal battle love.
- **Japan Twitter top 30 (Pokemon Co., 2021 via Nintendo Life):** no Gen 2 entry. Cute and odd commons from other gens dominate it, so the cute-common audience is real but not specific to Gen 2.
- **Sugimori, Nintendo Power, July 2000 (via Dr Lava):** "several hundred" new creatures made, most cut. New types were added to rebalance after Psychic dominance. Hoothoot came from a pet bird he saw standing on one leg. Later Gen 3 interview: none of the new creatures suited the very start of the game, so older ones were reused early. (Gamasutra: breeding and genders led to baby forms.)

## What the evidence says

1. Fire and water starters (Cyndaquil/Typhlosion, Totodile/Feraligatr) are loved for a readable silhouette plus a clear attitude: a small cute form that grows into a confident one.
2. Chikorita is a lesson in cute without payoff. It tops cuteness polls but its line (Bayleef, Meganium) is called plain and gets few votes.
3. The most loved Gen 2 creatures are not starters. Umbreon, Tyranitar, Lugia, Scizor are strongly themed with a limited palette and a single idea (night, armour, guardian).
4. Babies (Togepi, Pichu) are loved with only 2% shares each, comparable to the whole Chikorita line, with minimal detail: round, big head, small body.
5. Beloved commons (Marill, Wooper, Sentret, Hoothoot) are loved by recognisability: one-glance shapes, one signature trait (the Marill tail, the Wooper gills, Sentret upright on its tail, Hoothoot one-legged). Evidence for them is thinner (they place low in polls); their love shows in anime/merch appearances rather than rankings.

## Caveats

Polls are informal or skew toward battle strength; commons rarely appear. Search results did not surface Reddit threads about Sentret/Hoothoot.
