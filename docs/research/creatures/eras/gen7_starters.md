# Gen 7 (Alola) starters, regional forms and early commons

Scope: Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun/Moon, Let's Go. Principles only; we copy no creature.

## Evidence

Starter polls (Rowlet / Litten / Popplio):
- Nintendo reveal Twitter poll (2016, "most potential"): Rowlet 41%, Litten 38%, Popplio 21%.
- PokeCommunity thread: Rowlet 42.0%, Litten 35.2%, Popplio 22.7%.
- Nintendo World Report reader survey: Litten 47%, Rowlet 44%, Popplio 9%. Press Start: Litten 45%, Rowlet 36%.
- Pokejungle Starter Showdown (2022): Rowlet first, about 48% on Discord and Twitter.
- Takeaway: Rowlet most consistent winner, Litten wins smaller polls, Popplio usually last but its final form recovered.

Full-Gen-7 poll (Nintendo Life, 1,906 votes): Mimikyu 12%, Rowlet 9%, Decidueye 9%, Lycanroc 5%, Incineroar 4%, Primarina 4%, Litten 3%, Golisopod 3%, Popplio 2%. Final forms of all three starters beat or matched their bases, so the line paid off.

Official Pokemon of the Year fan vote (via Bulbapedia, as summarised in search results; the page itself returned 403 to me): Mimikyu is the top Gen 7 pick with roughly 99,000 votes and placed third overall in 2020 behind Greninja and Lucario (Gameranx). Togedemaru about 23rd with roughly 8,500 votes (year unclear, unverified).

Design commentary (Game Developer, Caleb Compton): starters follow a performer theme (archer, wrestler, opera singer), and Incineroar's Fire/Dark fits a villainous "heel" role. Commons use bright saturated colour and detailed but uncluttered shapes, avoiding arbitrary spots, rings, stripes and spikes of Gens 4-6. Details like Rowlet's leaf bowtie or Crabrawler's gloves express the concept without being on the nose. Regional forms are framed as island evolution, either softening the original (Alolan Raichu, Ninetales) or keeping the silhouette and adding details (Dugtrio, Golem, Muk, Marowak).

Mimikyu: a lonely creature in a homemade Pikachu costume; critics (Paste) read it as emotional vulnerability and a wink at Pikachu's mascot status. Popularity here is story-in-a-silhouette, not stats.

Alolan forms: Vulpix, Ninetales, Raichu (surfer), Marowak (Fire/Ghost, ranked first in a Sportskeeda list) and Exeggutor are the most discussed favourites; Exeggutor is loved as comic relief and as the setting's mascot. Geodude/Grimer lines drew complaints.

Disliked: Pikipek (Smogon JAPE: fails "head test", wears out its welcome), Yungoos (GameSkinny: too much like a real-world pest), Grubbin (compared unfavourably to Caterpie/Venipede), Pyukumuku (concept loved by some, "doesn't feel like a Pokemon" to others), Popplio's base form (nose, Oshawott resemblance).
Gap: I found no sourced data on Rattata/Meowth Alolan forms, Cutiefly, Rockruff or Bewear reception beyond the Nintendo Life poll rows.

## Principles
1. A one-sentence personality beats a stat line (Mimikyu, Incineroar the heel).
2. Final forms carry the line, but the base must be readable and cute enough to be picked.
3. Colour restraint: few saturated colours, one signature accessory.
4. Local theme makes commons feel part of a place (surf, wrestling, circus).
5. Reinterpreting a known creature with a new habitat story is cheap and loved.
6. Commons that mimic dull real animals (mongoose, woodpecker, grub) read as filler.
