# Pokemon Mystery Dungeon and Pokemon Ranger: personality-driven favourites

Research date: 2026-11-23. Scope: which creatures became loved as partners and characters, and why personality did it.

## Honest evidence status
The web-search budget was exhausted before any search ran, so I found NO poll with vote counts for PMD or Ranger partners. Only page fetches (Wikipedia) worked; Bulbapedia returned 403, Fandom 402. Everything below marked "verified" comes from fetched Wikipedia pages. Items marked "recollection" are from background knowledge and should be checked before we rely on them. ## Verified facts
- PMD sold over 17.26 million copies; Explorers games (Time/Darkness/Sky) sold 6.37 million, the best of the series; Rescue Team 5.85 million; Rescue Team DX 1.99 million (Wikipedia, Pokemon Mystery Dungeon).
- Wikipedia calls it the most popular Mystery Dungeon branch; the Pokemon cast is the draw, since the dungeon gameplay itself drew mixed scores (Metacritic 59-60 for Explorers, 69 for DX; IGN criticised repetitive gameplay "but found the story charming").
- Critics praised Explorers' story and music (GameSpot, Nintendo Power). So the love is for story and cast, not mechanics.
- Partner system: a personality quiz picks your Pokemon; you then pick a partner from 16 options, excluding the same type as you. Your partner is "a timid Pokemon who dreams of forming an exploration team", finds you on a beach, and grieves when you seem to die.
- Explorers cast: Grovyle (thief, ally, sacrifices himself), Dusknoir (trusted explorer revealed as villain), Celebi, Darkrai (Wikipedia, Explorers of Darkness/Time).
- Ranger: 2.93 million sold worldwide; Capture Styler loop-drawing; Ukulele Pichu becomes first partner after you repair its ukulele; partner Pokemon give field moves and Poke Assists; player starts with Minun or Plusle (Wikipedia, Pokemon Ranger and Guardian Signs). Guardian Signs sold about 1.53 million.
- Rescue Team DX: first-week Japan 138,548 physical copies, total 1.99 million.

## Recollection (unverified)
- Fan communities commonly name Grovyle, Dusknoir, Wigglytuff (guild master, goofy but secretly strong), Chatot, Loudred and the Team Skull style comic rivals, Sunflora, Munchlax, Gengar and partner Pokemon like Pikachu, Eevee, Riolu as PMD favourites.
- Charmander and Riolu partners are often cited for emotional weight; the Explorers ending is widely called a tearjerker.

## Why personality made them loved (analysis)
1. Role plus flaw: each guild member has a job and a quirk (loud, fussy, goofy, greedy), not just a type.
2. Stakes tied to the creature: Grovyle's sacrifice and Dusknoir's betrayal make two species memorable through one arc.
3. The partner speaks for itself: you see its fear, courage and grief, so a plain design becomes a person.
4. Choice with personality: the quiz makes you feel your pick says something about you.
5. Contrast of silhouette and attitude: gentle-looking creature, surprising bravery (Wigglytuff).
6. Ranger's version: bond through action; partners solve puzzles with their abilities, so utility = affection.
