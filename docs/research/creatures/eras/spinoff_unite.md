# Spin-offs: Pokemon Unite, Sleep, Cafe (mobile era)

Status: PARTIAL. The shared web search budget ran out before any search ran, so this note rests on four page fetches plus general knowledge. Anything marked (unverified) is not backed by a fetched source and should be re-checked before it drives a decision. No pick-rate, poll or skin-sales numbers were obtained.

## What the sources confirmed

- Pokemon Unite (Wikipedia): 9M Switch downloads by Sep 2021, 30M mobile by Oct 2021, 50M by Dec 2021, 70M Apr 2022, 80M Aug 2022, over 200M across platforms by Jul 2024. Metacritic 70/100 Switch. IGN 6/10 criticised microtransactions as pay-to-win. Players criticised cosmetic pricing, for example a US$40 Alolan Ninetales skin, with similar complaints about Halloween Lucario and Christmas Pikachu skins. Won Google Play Best Game 2021.
- Pokemon Unite official roster page: 79 playable Pokemon, filtered by role (Attacker, Speedster, All-Rounder, Defender, Supporter). No popularity data on the page. Roster includes many soft or round designs (Snorlax, Blissey, Sylveon, Alcremie, Comfey, Mimikyu, Psyduck, Greedent, Eldegoss, Azumarill, Pikachu) beside edgy ones.
- Pokemon Sleep (Wikipedia): released Jul 2023 by Select Button. Over 28M downloads and 1B sleep sessions by Jul 2025, 30M downloads announced Apr 2026. You start with one Snorlax that you feed; visitors arrive by sleep type and befriend you through biscuits; befriended Pokemon gather berries and ingredients. No revenue or reception detail was on the page.
- Pokemon Cafe ReMix (Wikipedia): free puzzle game, player and Eevee run a cafe. Metacritic 64/100 Switch. Destructoid: "one of the sweetest looking games available on mobile platforms." Critics praised the art and easy match mechanic; the pacing and life system drew criticism. Nominated Best Mobile Game, Game Awards 2020.

## Design principles that the confirmed facts support

1. A single caretaking relationship can carry a whole game. Pokemon Sleep is built around feeding one big sleepy creature, and it reached 30M downloads. One cozy anchor creature beats a wide roster of strangers.
2. Rewarding calm is a hook of its own. Sleep gives players a reason to open the game daily without pressure.
3. Charm is sold as the product in puzzle spin-offs. Cafe ReMix was praised mainly for being sweet to look at, even where gameplay was only average. Cute presentation alone earns goodwill.
4. Cosmetics are loved but price-sensitive. Unite players objected loudly to US$40 skins, so skins should feel like a gift or a cheap treat, not a gate.
5. Pay-to-win perception hurts reception more than any art issue.
6. Roster role clarity helps: five labelled roles let a large roster stay readable.
