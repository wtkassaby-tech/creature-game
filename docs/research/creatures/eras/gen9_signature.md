# Generation 9 (Paldea and Kitakami) signature designs: research notes

STATUS: UNVERIFIED. The web search budget for this run was used up (limit of 200 searches per turn, shared by every agent) before any search ran for this scope. Nothing below was checked against a source in this session. It comes from general background knowledge only. No polls, vote counts or sales numbers are quoted, because none could be confirmed. ## Designs fans commonly name as strong (to confirm)

- Lechonk line (Oinkologne): a round, soft pig with an instantly readable silhouette. Fans often point to it as an early-route favourite. Likely cause: a simple shape, big expressive face and a friendly role in the early game.
- Fuecoco line (Skeledirge): a goofy starter with a strong "personality" hook (the crocodile that sings). The final form's skull-and-candle motif is a clear theme. Likely cause: comedy at stage 1 and a dramatic payoff at stage 3.
- Sprigatito line (Meowscarada): a cat starter that became a mascot in merchandise. The final form's magician theme was widely discussed. Likely cause: very cute stage 1 and a style-driven stage 3.
- Quaxly line (Quaquaval): a duckling to a dancer. Likely cause: a clear concept that changes with each stage.
- Tinkatink line (Tinkaton): a small, bold-colour hammer-wielder, often called a standout mid-game find. Likely cause: a clear tool-as-body idea, a strong silhouette and a very visible gameplay role.
- Ceruledge / Armarouge (from Charcadet): a rival-pair evolution choice with a strong colour contrast. Likely cause: a simple "pick a side" story and stylish shapes.
- Gholdengo: a coin-covered ghost that became a meme and a competitive staple. Likely cause: a one-line concept (gold coins), a clean shape and strong attention online.
- Pawmi line, Fidough line, Wiglett, Flittle: small, simple, cute creatures that fans often cite for charm. Likely cause: a single readable trait each.
- Kitakami (Teal Mask): Ogerpon, with its mask-swapping idea, and Fezandipiti/Okidogi/Munkidori (the three "Loyal Three"). Mixed reception for the trio, stronger for Ogerpon. Likely cause: the mask mechanic doubled as a design hook.

## Designs often criticised (to confirm)

- Bellibolt: a frog-like creature called awkward and bloated.
- Squawkabilly: a parrot whose varied colour forms were liked less than its function.
- Tauros Paldea forms: a simple recolour and tweak that some saw as lazy.
- Some paradox creatures (e.g. Roaring Moon, Iron Valiant): split opinion, from loved to dismissed as a "retro/future" re-skin of an old design.
- Several late-route single-stage creatures: seen as under-designed.

## Recurring traits (hypotheses)

1. One-sentence concept (coins, singing, hammer, mask).
2. Clear silhouette that reads at small size.
3. A stage-1 that is cute or funny, with a stage-3 that is dramatic.
4. A strong colour contrast, often two or three main colours.
5. A tool or object as part of the body.
6. Rival-pair evolutions (choose a side).
7. Fits a visible gameplay job.
8. Easily turned into merchandise (round, plush-friendly forms).

## Lessons for ClayPals (hypotheses)

- Give every part a one-line concept so a baby that mixes parts still reads.
- Keep silhouettes chunky so mixed parts stay legible.
- Limit each creature to two or three colours.
- Make a clay-toy "plush-friendly" form the baseline.
- Avoid pure recolours of old designs.
