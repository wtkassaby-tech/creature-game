# Legends Z-A and 2025-2026 designs: signature creatures and Mega forms

Status: PARTIAL. The shared web-search budget ran out before this scope's searches ran, so only one source was read (Wikipedia, via fetch). Bulbapedia returned 403. Everything marked [unverified] is background knowledge that needs a poll or source before it informs a decision.

## Verified evidence (Wikipedia, Pokemon Legends: Z-A)
- Sales: over 5.8 million units in week one; 1.48 million in Japan in four days; 13.31 million by 30 Sep 2026 (9.27M Switch, 4.04M Switch 2).
- Reception: Metacritic 70 (Switch) and 78 (Switch 2); OpenCritic 66% recommend; the Mega Dimension DLC 56%. Critics praised the battle system and criticised the single-city setting and flat visuals. So the creatures were not the complaint; the world was.
- Mega forms for the Kalos starters were shown in a September 2025 Direct. Mega Dimension adds two Mega forms for Raichu (the third Pokemon with two Megas).
- Reaction to leaks: Mega Starmie's legs were widely mocked ("goofy", "cursed" headlines in Polygon and The Escapist, as cited by Wikipedia). Fans also complained when Flygon was missing from a leaked Mega list. Both show fans react most strongly to a beloved silhouette being altered or left out.

## Design observations [unverified, from general knowledge]
- Megas that keep the base silhouette and exaggerate one signature feature (a crest, a cape, claws, a tail) tend to be praised; Megas that bolt on unrelated parts or change leg proportions tend to be mocked.
- Regional signature creatures that fans name as best of a generation share simple, readable shapes, a strong colour pair and one memorable feature.

## Principles for ClayPals
1. Protect the silhouette: a variant or evolved form should be recognisable as a solid black shape. The Starmie backlash is what happens when leg and body proportions break.
2. Exaggerate one feature rather than adding many. Our part-based breeding already swaps head, body, legs and tail, so give each slot one clear hero feature.
3. Fans notice who was left out. Rarity and breeding should let favoured "classic" shapes appear often enough that players feel represented.
4. Great creatures do not rescue a thin world: Z-A's creatures sold well but critics blamed setting and visuals. Keep biomes varied.
5. Variants that give a creature a second look (two Megas for Raichu) get attention; our cosmetic variants can use the same "second take" idea without new systems.
6. Leaks drive discussion: show breeding results early and often so players share their own mixes.
