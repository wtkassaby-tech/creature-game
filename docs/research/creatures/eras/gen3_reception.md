# Generation 3 (Hoenn) fan reception: notes for ClayPals

Scope: Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed/LeafGreen. Principles only; we copy no creature. Polls are informal unless stated.

## Hard numbers

- Official fan vote, Pokemon Company x Google, Feb 2020, over 6.6 million votes: Rayquaza 8th overall (60,939 votes) and top Hoenn; Gardevoir 9th overall (60,596), second in Hoenn; Blaziken 5th in Hoenn (38,307), not top 10 overall. Totals differ slightly between outlets (Bulbapedia's own page returned 403 for me, so Hoenn figures come via a search summary; verify before quoting).
- "Every Pokemon is Someone's Favorite" fan site (257,900+ votes at time of article): Absol 2,218 votes, 4th most favourited overall, the top new Gen 3 Pokemon, ahead of Mudkip (ComicBook.com).
- Nintendo Life Hoenn poll, 16 Feb 2026, 2,424 votes: Rayquaza 9%, Gardevoir 7%, Mudkip 6%, Blaziken 5%, Flygon 5%, then Swampert, Metagross, Absol 4%, then Aggron, Milotic, Sceptile 3%. Very flat spread: no runaway favourite.
- Charlie Intel poll: Gen 3 won "favourite generation" with about 3.2k votes; commenters said it mostly tracks the game people started with (nostalgia effect).
- Sales: Ruby/Sapphire about 16.2 million, Emerald roughly 6.3 to 7 million (sources disagree), over 5 million in Japan alone by 2003. Remakes Omega Ruby/Alpha Sapphire about 14.3 to 14.6 million, so the region sustained demand.
- Mudkip meme: fanart group on DeviantArt from Oct 2004, "I herd u liek mudkips" phrase from early 2005, spread via 4chan, used on Anonymous protest signs in 2008 (Bulbanews, Know Your Meme). A cute, simple, slightly goofy face became a meme that outlived the game.

## What the designers said (Nintendo Dream 2003 interview, translated by Lava Cut)

- Sugimori wanted designs "cooler and less childish" after fans called the series babyish, and wanted Gen 3 to feel new after Gold/Silver reused too much.
- Eight designers worked on the Pokemon; Sugimori finalised each in his own style so the set stayed coherent.
- Blaziken was his first design to make the final game, partly a test of whether players would accept a humanoid Pokemon.
- The in-house testers repeatedly said new designs "don't look like Pokemon." The team shipped them anyway; the humanoid and monster-style ones became favourites.
- Torchic's line was based on real chicks: cute first, less cute grown up. Wurmple was made a Caterpie-like shape so evolution was easy for new players to grasp.

## Most mocked (design reasons)

- Luvdisc: "a sideways heart", looks unfinished or lazy (TheGamer).
- Shelgon: awkward middle stage that does not look like the line it sits in.
- Spinda: random spot pattern reads as dizzy and confused.
- Swalot, Spoink, Whiscash, Seedot, Nosepass, Chimecho, Volbeat/Illumise, Kecleon, Huntail, Grumpig also appear on "worst" lists (TheGamer, TechRaptor, Nintendo World Report, PokeCommunity). One NWR writer called Hoenn designs the worst in the series; other posters call them the best, so the generation is polarising.
- Common complaint words: forgettable, lazy, strange, gross.

## Takeaways

1. The loved set is mostly bold silhouettes with a strong single idea: a legendary, a cool biped, a graceful humanoid, a nocturnal beast.
2. Cute-simple starters (Mudkip) win by face and expression, which also made them meme material.
3. Polarising or "doesn't look like our usual creature" can still become a favourite if the shape is strong.
4. Failures are thin concepts: one object or shape with little character, or a stage that breaks the line's visual continuity.
5. Fan love is spread over many creatures, so variety and a clear identity per creature matter more than one hero.
