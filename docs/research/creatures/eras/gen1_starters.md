# Gen 1 (Kanto): starters and early commons

Research for ClayPals. Design principles only; nothing here is to be copied.

## Evidence

**Reddit survey, 2019 (52,725 votes, 809 species, self-selected r/pokemon users).** Gen 1 got 18,289 votes, about 121 per species, the highest of any generation (Gen 2: 91, Gen 3: 70). Top Gen 1: Charizard 1,107; Gengar 1,056; Arcanine 923; Bulbasaur 710; Eevee 581; Dragonite 551; Squirtle 523; Ninetales 471; Snorlax 433. Pikachu ranked 46th. Source: Nintendo Life write-up.

**Nintendo Life Gen 1 poll (5,645 votes).** Charizard 7%, Gengar 6%, Bulbasaur 5%, Squirtle 4%; Charmander, Blastoise, Pikachu, Arcanine, Eevee, Snorlax, Dragonite 3% each. Early-route filler (Weedle, Kakuna, Ekans, Nidoran, Golbat, Machop, Doduo) got 0%. Clefairy 0.4%. Commenters said favourites tracked their first-playthrough team.

**Starter poll (Nintendo Life, 2020).** Charmander took the Fire category over Cyndaquil (24%) and Torchic (13%); the article credits Charizard for lifting it. Reader comments (unverified) said Gen 1 won all three categories.

**Disliked, Japan 2016 (500 people, via My Nintendo News).** Grimer, Muk and Weezing were the three least appealing; 8 of the bottom 10 were Gen 1.

**Designer and design-history sources.** Sugimori's stated goal (2000 interview, translated) was kaiju-like monsters that fight on your side. Gen 1 skewed tough and brutish, with cute designs added later; Clefairy is reportedly the one "cute" Gen 1 design Sugimori made. Pikachu was designed by Atsuko Nishida as a "cute monster"; Sugimori set the yellow and back stripes (Yomiuri via ResetEra, secondhand). Game Developer analysis: Game Boy limits meant one main colour plus a contrasting accent, simple silhouettes, repeated motifs (angry vs cute eyes, three-claw hands, triangular ears), and light bellies on about 40 species.

## Reading the evidence

- Starters and their final forms dominate. The top of every poll is starter lines plus a few "cool" non-starters (Gengar, Arcanine, Dragonite). Personal history (first team) strongly biases votes, so treat rankings as partly nostalgia.
- Beloved non-starters share a pattern: a clear animal or archetype, a strong silhouette, and one standout trait (Gengar's grin, Arcanine's mane, Snorlax's bulk).
- Pikachu is a merchandise and mascot success more than a poll winner among fans (46th in the Reddit survey, 3% in Nintendo Life), so "cute and simple" sells broadly but is not what dedicated fans rank highest.
- Early-route commons mostly score near zero in polls. Evidence for "beloved commons" is thin in these sources; they are loved as memories (Pidgey, Rattata) rather than as designs. I did not find numeric data on this; a follow-up on the bug and bird lines is needed.

## Gaps

No direct interview material on individual designs (e.g. Rhydon) was found. Merchandise sales numbers were not retrieved. The Reddit survey allowed multiple votes per person, so counts are not clean first choices.
