# Gen 9 (Scarlet/Violet, Teal Mask, Indigo Disk): legendaries, mythicals, mascots

Status: PARTIAL. The shared web-search budget ran out before any search ran, so only four Wikipedia pages were fetched. Items marked (unverified) come from background knowledge, not from a source checked this session. Re-run with search budget for poll numbers.

## What the sources confirmed
- Scarlet/Violet sold over 10M copies in 3 days (4.05M in Japan), the best 3-day launch of any Nintendo platform software; 27.15M by June 2025. Metacritic 72/71, the lowest of the mainline games; OpenCritic 48% recommend. The creature lineup sold the game while technical polish hurt it.
- Koraidon and Miraidon are the box-art mascots. They are Paradox creatures (past / future), their names are Japanese "korai"/"mirai" plus a dinosaur "-don", and the player rides them as mounts. The Wikipedia list credits their story development as praised.
- Ogerpon (Teal Mask) is an oni-inspired masked creature. Its typing changes with the mask it holds, and the story rival (Kieran) idolises it and drifts from the player after capture.
- Terapagos (Indigo Disk) is a turtle with three forms, and catching it unlocks the Stellar Tera type. Pecharunt is a peach creature based on the Momotaro folk tale, a timed mythical.
- Ting-Lu, Chien-Pao, Wo-Chien and Chi-Yu are four sealed "Treasures of Ruin" (deer, snow leopard, moss snail, goldfish) unlocked by finding stakes: a collect-the-seals quest.
- Tinkaton has a documented positive reception; its hammer-upgrade backstory (revenge on bullying Steel types) is a character hook.
- Named designers on the generation: Mari Shimazaki (Fidough), Yusuke Kozaki (Varoom, Cetoddle), James Turner (Dondozo, Paldean Tauros). Outside designers brought fresh silhouettes.
- Fuecoco's vacant expression made it a fan favourite (Inside Games polls); Flamigo was called bland yet won through competitive strength.

## Background knowledge (unverified)
- Ogerpon is widely regarded as the best-loved Gen 9 legendary; Miraidon was strong in competitive play; Koraidon and Miraidon drew mixed reactions for resembling a lesser Pokemon (Cyclizar) and for losing their power in the story.
- Pecharunt and the Ruin quartet drew lukewarm responses (Pecharunt's unlock was a tedious chore).

## Design lessons
1. A mascot that carries a story arc beats a mascot that is only a stat block.
2. A creature that changes with a held item (Ogerpon's masks) gives one design many looks, which suits our part-swap breeding.
3. Cultural folk-tale roots give instant legibility; invent original ones.
4. The legendary is the quest, not only the creature: sealed shrines gave a hunt.
5. Mounts make a creature a companion you see all day.
6. A big roster sold the game even when technical quality was poor, but legendaries must feel earned, not chores.
