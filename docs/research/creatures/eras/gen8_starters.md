# Generation 8 (Galar, Hisui, Sinnoh remakes): starters and early commons

Status: PARTIAL. The shared web-search budget ran out after the first batch, so only two Wikipedia pages were read. Items marked (recalled) come from general knowledge, not from a source fetched this session. Re-verify before relying on them.

## Verified evidence
- Starter poll (official Pokemon Twitter account, pre-release, per Wikipedia's Gen 8 list): Sobble 38%, Scorbunny 37%, Grookey 25%. Close to a three-way split, with the timid water type narrowly ahead.
- Wooloo: the director, Shigeru Ohmori, was surprised by the reaction; he had seen it as "just another Pokemon in the mix." It became an informal mascot for Sword/Shield and spread to the anime, Pokemon GO, the card game, plushies and Funko Pops. Tumblr's 2019 year-end list put Wooloo second among most-discussed subjects, behind only Pikachu.
- Critics: IGN's Janet Garcia called it the cutest Pokemon in the games; Polygon's Michael McWhertor called it "perfect in every way", crediting the round shape and friendly nature; Fanbyte read its anxiety as relatable and the design as built for emotional connection; CBR used it to show how Normal-types are loved individually but overshadowed.
- Galarian Corsola was widely written about (CNET, Polygon, HuffPost) as a climate-change / coral-bleaching image: a design with a story attached.
- Alcremie (including Gigantamax) was praised as cute (Time, Eater coverage).
- Obstagoon resembles Gene Simmons of Kiss, who called it flattering: a silhouette with personality.
- Legends: Arceus sold 6.5 million in week one and 14.83 million by 31 March 2023. Eurogamer praised how vividly the wild Pokemon are animated.

## Recalled, not verified this session
- Early-route Galar commons that fans commonly cite as beloved: Wooloo, Skwovet (a chubby, food-hoarding squirrel), Yamper (a corgi that carries a ball), Nickit, Gossifleur. Their appeal is a simple, readable animal with one behaviour.
- Commonly disliked: Sobble's final form Inteleon is often called too edgy or lanky compared with its cute first stage; Dracovish is disliked for its odd half-fossil premise; Drednaw is sometimes called generic. Treat as opinion, not data.
- Cinderace and Rillaboom are widely discussed as strong designs with a clear "sport/music" theme (football, drum).

## Design principles (supported by the verified items)
1. Simple, rounded silhouettes with an instantly readable real-world animal win hearts (Wooloo).
2. A relatable emotion or behaviour (anxiety, shyness) beats complexity.
3. The cutest-looking stage 1 can outpoll the stage 3; final evolutions risk losing the charm that made the starter.
4. A design that carries a real-world story (Corsola) gets press without any marketing.
5. Commons can become mascots if they are plain, friendly and memeable.
6. Ordinary Normal-type-style creatures are loved precisely because they are plain.
