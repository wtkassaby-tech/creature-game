# Generation 5 (Unova): signature designs and what we can learn

Scope: Black/White and Black 2/White 2, an all-new roster of 156 creatures. Method: web research (polls, interviews, retrospectives). Evidence quality is mixed: only one poll gave usable numbers, and several sites blocked fetching (Ranker, TechRaptor), so those are cited from search summaries only.

## Hard evidence
- Nintendo Life Gen 5 favourites poll, 2,016 votes: Chandelure 8%, Zoroark 5%, Volcarona 5%, Oshawott 4%, Krookodile 4%, Hydreigon 4%, Victini 3%, Excadrill 3%, Haxorus 3%. The top is flat (the leader has 8%), so love is spread over many creatures, but the leaders skew to the dramatic and the mid-game.
- Google's Pokemon of the Year poll (via TheGamer): no Gen 5 creature reached the top 10. The best was Chandelure at 17th. The author suggests the very large roster split the vote.
- Ranker's Gen 5 list (about 161,000 votes per search summary) names Landorus as the most divisive entry. Unverified because the page was blocked.
- Disliked picks are consistent across outlets: Trubbish/Garbodor, the Vanillite line (Vanilluxe), Klink, Stunfisk.

## Design intent (Iwata Asks and Nintendo Dream/Power translations, via secondary write-ups)
- All-new roster, so the team "went back to animals" and avoided designs whose source is obvious at a glance.
- Masuda asked for "cool", so lines tend to be sharp and angular; Sugimori acted as the brake.
- About 17 designers made roughly 10 each. Sugimori says about three times more designs were made than used (Lava Cut estimates 400-500 for 156), so heavy cutting.
- Selection test: fierce enough to fight, appealing enough to befriend. Type and animal-group balance cut strong designs.
- Starters were tied to the region's cultures (Japan, West, China), so a concept hook was baked in.
- A retrospective (Bogleech) praises the generation for dropping excess markings, unnecessary joints and sharp angles, with anatomy that makes sense. It cites Reuniclus, Ferrothorn, Scrafty and Golurk as inventive.

## What the mid-game favourites share
- Chandelure, Zoroark, Volcarona, Excadrill, Krookodile, Haxorus, Hydreigon: each reads from one strong silhouette idea (a candelabra flame, a fox with a mane, a moth with a sun-wing, a drill-claw mole, a crocodile with a wrapped scarf, an axe-tusked dragon, a three-headed beast).
- Each has one signature part that carries the identity (head, wings, claws, tusks). Breeding is part-based, so that maps directly onto our parts.
- Most are the final stage of a 2- or 3-stage line, a payoff for effort.
- A story or mood hook (ghost lamp that burns spirits, illusion fox) makes the design memorable beyond looks.

## Why the disliked ones fail
- A literal object with a face and limbs added (garbage bag, ice cream cone, gear) reads as a joke or lazy; the object overpowers any creature feel.
- Weak or incoherent silhouettes: a flat flounder (Stunfisk) fails the "what is it" test.
- The perceived cause given by critics is quantity over quality, and the biggest roster in the series dilutes attention.

## Caveats
- Mid-game fan lists are mostly opinion; the only numeric poll is one site. Do not over-read percentages.
- Objects can work when they are treated as creatures first (Gen 5 fans do like Golurk, Chandelure, Klinklang with some caveats), so the lesson is about treatment, not a ban.
