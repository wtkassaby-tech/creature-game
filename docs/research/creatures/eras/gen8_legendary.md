# Gen 8 (Galar, Hisui, Sinnoh remakes): legendaries, mascots, box art

STATUS: PARTIAL. The shared web-search budget ran out before any search ran. Only two Wikipedia pages were read. Everything marked (recall) is from model memory and is NOT verified; re-check before relying on it. No poll vote counts were found beyond the one below.

## Verified evidence
- Starter poll (Pokemon Company Twitter, per Wikipedia gen VIII list): Sobble 38%, Scorbunny 37%, Grookey 25%. A near three-way split means all three read as lovable; the small water lizard won.
- Wooloo: Polygon's Michael McWhertor called it "perfect in every way"; director Shigeru Ohmori said its popularity surprised the team; heavy fan art.
- Yamper: called adorable at reveal (Game Informer, Time).
- Corviknight: described as a quick fan favourite (no figures).
- Disliked: Impidimp called a "monstrosity" (Game Informer); Rolycoly criticised over its backstory (Game Revolution); Alcremie flagged for innuendo in dex text (Eater, Time).
- Zacian was noted to resemble Sif from Dark Souls (a wolf-knight silhouette people already know).
- Legends: Arceus sold 6.5 million in week one and 14.83 million by 31 March 2023. It had 17 new regional forms. The Hisuian Zorua ONA won a Webby People's Voice award, so a regional form carried its own story.

## Unverified recall (needs checking)
- Zacian/Zamazenta: dog/wolf with a sword or shield in the mouth, a clear box-art pair with one-glance roles. Reception was warm on the look; the cheap "crowned" form change got more criticism (recall).
- Eternatus: Gigantamax-style dragon, with many saying the final boss form beat the base form (recall).
- Calyrex: rider and steed riders in the DLC, praised as a fresh concept (recall).
- Urshifu: a martial-arts tiger cub trained into two styles, widely liked (recall).
- Hisuian Zoroark and Hisuian Typhlosion: ghost-themed regional forms often cited as favourites (recall).
- Gigantamax: mixed; some forms were big, silly and loved, many were seen as just "bigger" (recall).
- Critics said Galar's legendary lore felt thin and the story too short (recall).

## Design principles (tentative)
1. Everyday animal first, then a twist (Wooloo, Corviknight, Hisuian forms).
2. Pair legendaries by mirrored roles so the box art tells the story at a glance.
3. A regional or variant form with a story hook can outsell a brand-new species for attachment.
4. Cute-and-simple beat clever-and-busy; the busiest designs (Alcremie, Impidimp) drew the criticism.
5. A transformation should look different, not just bigger.
6. Lore and the way you meet a legendary matter as much as the model.
