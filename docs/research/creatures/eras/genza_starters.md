## What was verified (Wikipedia, "Pokemon Legends: Z-A", fetched 2026-11-23)
- Reception: Metacritic 70/100 (Switch) and 78/100 (Switch 2); OpenCritic 66% recommend.
- Sales: over 5.8 million in the first week; 13.31 million by 30 September 2026 (9.27M Switch, 4.04M Switch 2).
- Critics praised the battle system. Some called the Lumiose City setting visually restrictive. Nintendo Life called the visuals "lifeless and flat".
- The Mega Dimension DLC (10 December 2025) added new Mega forms. A leak reported 16 Pokemon with Megas, and leaked Mega Starmie drew strong fan reaction. This is a reaction report, not a vote count.
- The fetch did not mention Chikorita, Tepig or Totodile or any starter evolution coverage. The page was only 100,000 of ~127,000 characters read.

## Design hypotheses worth testing (from general knowledge, not evidence)
1. Megas reward a design that is already loved, then exaggerate its silhouette.
2. Early commons stay loved when they have a clear one-shape read and a small expressive trait.
