# Generation 4 (Sinnoh): starters, evolution lines, early-route commons

Research notes for ClayPals. Design principles only; no creature is to be copied. Figures come from web search summaries (Bulbapedia's 2020 Pokemon of the Year table could not be fetched directly, 403), so treat exact vote counts as secondhand.

## Evidence

**Official poll (Pokemon of the Year, 2020, via Bulbapedia's Sinnoh table):** Lucario 102,259 votes (top of Gen 4, second overall); Garchomp 61,877; Luxray 46,032; Piplup 34,680; Infernape 33,267; Empoleon 21,773 (ninth in the generation). Starters are not the top of their own generation; two non-starter, non-legendary "pseudo-mascot" designs beat them by 2-3x.

**Starter fan polls (small, self-selected forums):**
- Bulbagarden weekly poll (~75 voters): Piplup 41.3%, Turtwig 36.0%, Chimchar 22.7%.
- Bulbagarden "which starter will you choose" (95): Piplup 37.9%, Turtwig 36.8%, Chimchar 25.3%.
- PokeCommunity (146): Piplup 62 votes, Chimchar 60, Turtwig 39.
- Verdict: a near three-way split, Piplup narrowly ahead at the first-choice stage. Chimchar tends to gain at the final-form stage (Infernape 33k vs Empoleon 22k officially). Looper's cross-generation survey gave the Sinnoh trio only 11.33% of "best starters", so the trio is respected, not dominant.
- Fan polls (Reborn Evo, Bulbagarden): Lucario first; Infernape and Staraptor tied (56 each) ahead of Empoleon (46); Torterra leads starters in one thread, with individual fans calling it a lifelong favourite.

**Early-route commons:** Starly (Route 201/202), Bidoof (everywhere early), Shinx (Route 202), Budew (Route 204, levels 4-5). Game8 and Sportskeeda describe Starly as a team staple into the late game (Staraptor ties Infernape in fan polls). Bidoof became a meme: players needed an HM carrier, saw it constantly in the early game, and its cry stuck; ironic "Bidoof god" jokes escalated until the Pokemon Company embraced it (Bidoof Day, more Bidoof in Pokemon GO, Rick Astley parody video, Pokemon Snap hero coverage by Vice). Source: PokeBase, mein-mmo, Vice, Game Informer, Know Your Meme.

**Cross-gen evolutions:** Gamasutra's Gen 4 design series counts 29 evolutions or pre-evolutions of older creatures and notes designs became more detailed, "even cluttered", with four or more major colours the norm (previously rare). Forum favourites: Togekiss, Magnezone, Leafeon, Glaceon, Porygon-Z, Froslass, Gallade, Yanmega, Mamoswine, Mismagius. Frequent complaints: Honchkrow, Rhyperior, Dusknoir, Electivire, Probopass, Ambipom (humanised, shallower than the original concept); Happiny called unnecessary.

## What the loved ones share
1. A clear one-line silhouette read (Lucario's jackal-with-aura, Garchomp's shark-dragon fin-and-claw outline).
2. Pre-evolution cuteness that matures into a cool or dignified final form (Riolu to Lucario, Gible to Garchomp, Shinx to Luxray).
3. A strong "mascot" role: a design the player trusts with a story or rival slot.
4. Power fantasy plus expressive face/eyes.
5. Early availability: Starly and Bidoof are seen constantly, so familiarity builds affection.
6. Humour and personality in the ordinary: Bidoof's blank face and cry made a plain "filler" creature into the most-memed one.
7. Stage 1 or 2 designs that already work, not only the final form.
8. Evolutions that expand an existing idea rather than replace it (Togekiss, Leafeon).

## Lessons for ClayPals
1. Make the common early creature a character, not filler. Bidoof proves a round, plain, silly-faced animal can become the most beloved thing in the region if it is seen constantly and has a distinct sound and job.
2. Give every early route a "workhorse" creature that stays useful late (Starly to Staraptor), so players never feel they are discarding it.
3. Design the baby stage to be the cutest and the final stage the most dignified; for breeding, make sure mixed parts keep a readable silhouette. Keep silhouette simple: part-swapped babies must still read as one animal.
4. Avoid clutter. Four-plus colours on one creature is the main criticism of Gen 4; in clay, 2-3 colours and chunky shapes keep mixes readable.
5. Do not humanise an evolution at the cost of the original idea; mixed-part breeding should respect the base animal.
6. Expect starters to split opinion about evenly three ways; make each choice a different personality, not a power rank, and give the "cool" final form to at least one.
7. Offer an HM-style job (carrier, digger, swimmer) for plain creatures; utility makes memes, and memes make loyalty.

## Disliked (design reasons)
Honchkrow, Rhyperior, Dusknoir, Electivire, Probopass, Ambipom: humanised or lumpy compared with the pre-evolution; Happiny: seen as an unnecessary add-on.
