# Generation 4 (Sinnoh) fan reception: notes for ClayPals

Scope: Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold/SoulSilver. Principles only; we copy no creature.

## Evidence gathered

**Official vote (strongest data).** The 2020 Pokemon of the Year vote (The Pokemon Company with Google), per SoraNews24 and Bulbapedia:
- Lucario: 2nd overall, 102,259 votes, top Gen 4 creature.
- Garchomp: 7th overall, 61,877 votes, 2nd among Gen 4.
- Context: Greninja 1st with 140,599; Pikachu only 19th with 48,060. SoraNews24 noted that players who play the games favoured battle-ready final evolutions over pure cuteness, and that Eevee's vote was split across its evolutions (about 236,856 combined).

**Fan poll (small).** Nintendo Life "favourite Gen 4 Pokemon", about 2,200 votes: Lucario 9%, Garchomp 7%, Piplup 5%, Luxray 4%, then Togekiss, Leafeon, Glaceon, Darkrai, Empoleon, Torterra, Infernape at 3% each. Chatot, Lopunny and Spiritomb under 1%. Takeaway: a flat distribution; only two clear leaders, and a long tail of starters and Eeveelutions.

**A comicbook.com / fan-site generational poll** (about 1,900 votes) also put Lucario first for Gen 4. Gen 4 placed second or third as a whole generation in a separate fan poll (Gen 3 won).

**Most mocked.**
- TheGamer's list of weak Gen 4 designs: Bibarel (teeth and vacant eyes, an HM slave), Ambipom (tail-hands, "nightmare"), Glameow/Purugly (springy tail, evolution with no clear reason), Hippopotas, Lumineon (forgettable), Happiny (existing only as a breeding gate), Bronzor (looks like a decorated plate), Cherrim (boring base form relying on a gimmick), Kricketune.
- Techraptor's worst-of-Gen-4 list also opens with Bibarel.
- A Bulbagarden forum post argues Sinnoh was the last region with a pool of deliberately weak early-game creatures.

**Memes.** Bidoof became a meme because it was the default HM carrier in Diamond/Pearl (Bibarel learns 7 of 8 HMs). The company embraced it: more Bidoof in Pokemon GO from 2018, "Bidoof Day" on 1 July, heavy use in New Pokemon Snap and the remakes. Fan fatigue exists too: an Escapist list of most disliked Pokemon (ranked by X/Twitter mention volume, no vote counts) put Bidoof 8th of 10, citing overexposure in Legends: Arceus.

**Cross-gen evolutions.** Gen 4 added evolutions for older creatures (Electivire, Magmortar, Togekiss, Weavile and others). Competitive coverage found: Electivire peaked on rain teams, then fell. I found no reliable reception polling for these, so treat their reception as unquantified.

## Gaps
No sales figures, fan-art counts, or Sugimori/Turner interview found in this pass. Rotom and Togekiss reception was not documented by sources found.

## Lessons
1. Final forms that look strong and readable beat pure cute: the top vote went to a sleek bipedal hybrid.
2. Hybrid silhouettes mixing several animals read as new and mythic (fans attribute Anubis, jackal, canine to Lucario) while staying simple to recognise.
3. The disliked list is mostly early-route filler and awkward evolutions. Every creature a player meets early needs a clear identity.
4. A creature with no appeal can still become beloved through role: Bidoof's mechanical usefulness made it a meme. Give throwaway creatures a funny role, not just weak stats.
5. Evolutions or changes with no visible reason (Glameow/Purugly, Happiny) draw scorn. Change a part visibly and meaningfully.
6. Gimmick-dependent base forms (Cherrim) feel boring when the gimmick is off.
7. Votes split across families; plan a few clear hero lines.
