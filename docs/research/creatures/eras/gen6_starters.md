# Gen 6 (Kalos): starters and early commons

Scope: X/Y/ORAS starters (Chespin, Fennekin, Froakie lines) and early-route commons. Research on 23 Nov 2026. Evidence is a mix of official/large polls and weaker fan lists; strength noted per item.

## Evidence with numbers
- Greninja topped Japan's 2016 general election of all 720 Pokemon: 36,235 votes from 562,386 voters (Kotaku report via search; page itself not re-fetched). It also won the 2020 Pokemon of the Year vote (about 140k-150k votes, sources disagree on the exact figure).
- Nintendo Life Gen 6 poll, 1,535 votes: Greninja 15%, Aegislash 8%, Sylveon 7%, Tyrantrum 5%, Hawlucha 5%, Noivern 4%.
- Tiermaker tier list built from 4,000+ fan lists: Froakie is the top-rated Gen 6 starter (B tier), Fennekin the only C tier entry (as summarised by SVG/Looper-style coverage; secondary source).
- Fan vote of the best Pokemon per generation (ComicBook): Sylveon for Gen 6. Same site's least-favourite vote: Binacle for Gen 6.
- TheGamer "most adorable X/Y" list (editorial, not a poll): Sylveon, Fennekin, Helioptile, Fletchling, Flabebe, Chespin, Phantump, Pancham, Swirlix, Froakie.
- Game Developer analysis: Gen 6 had only 72 new Pokemon, softer silhouettes ("almost no sharp lines"), rounder simpler eyes closer to western cartoons (Quilladin, Bunnelby, Litleo), starters mapped to classic RPG archetypes (knight, rogue, mage).

## What we learn
1. The most loved starter line is the one whose final form is a clear role fantasy (ninja frog) with a distinctive silhouette, not the cutest baby. Greninja's popularity lifts Froakie; Fennekin is cuter as a baby but its final form is weaker.
2. The anime and games gave Greninja story and gameplay presence (Ash-Greninja, Smash fighter). Attachment grows from use, not just looks. Design alone does not explain it.
3. Cute early commons win on a single readable idea: tiny robin (Fletchling), flower on a baby (Flabebe), bamboo-chewing panda with attitude (Pancham), big-eared puppy-lizard (Helioptile).
4. Contrast between baby and adult is loved when the adult adds menace (Fletchling to Talonflame) but can split fans (Fennekin to Delphox).
5. Simple, rounded, big-eyed forms read well on small 3D models; they are what our clay style already does.
6. Gimmick-driven or object-fused designs and "just a pile of stuff" designs top dislike lists (Binacle, Klefki, Slurpuff criticisms).
7. Extra detail can hurt a good base (Froakie's nose bubbles criticised).
8. Mixed-theme region identity (France, fairy tales) gave coherence; fans still called the region a disappointment because the roster was small and the early cast samey.
