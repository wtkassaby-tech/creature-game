# Gen 5 (Unova): legendaries, mascots and box-art creatures

Scope: Black/White and Black 2/White 2. Evidence is thinner for legendaries than for regular Pokemon, which is itself a finding. Search tools were limited this session (no per-creature poll data for several legendaries); gaps are marked.

## Hard numbers

- Nintendo Life Gen 5 favourite poll (2,016 votes, 156 Pokemon): Chandelure 8%, Zoroark 5%, Volcarona 5%, Hydreigon 4%, Krookodile 4%, Oshawott 4%, Haxorus 3%, Excadrill 3%. Legendaries trail badly: Victini 3% (best legendary), Zekrom 2%, Reshiram 1%, Meloetta 1%, Genesect 0.9%, Kyurem 0.8%, Keldeo 0.8%, Landorus 0.3%, Cobalion/Virizion 0.2%, Terrakion 0.1%, Tornadus/Thundurus 0.1%. (Counts approximate, from rounded percentages.)
- Pokemon of the Year poll (Feb, per-region picks): no Gen 5 Pokemon in the overall top 10 (Greninja, Lucario, Mimikyu, Charizard, Umbreon, Sylveon, Garchomp, Rayquaza, Gardevoir, Gengar). Chandelure was the best Unova entry at 17th. Author suggests 155 Unova Pokemon split the vote.
- ComicBook.com fan vote of least popular per generation: Tornadus (16 votes) and Thundurus (18) were the least liked Gen 5 Pokemon.
- Nintendo Life "no votes" poll: Gen 5 had the most zero-vote Pokemon (14).

## What the designers said

- Iwata Asks: 17 people worked on new Pokemon designs. Sugimori says they design about three times more than they use; fan translations of Nintendo Power suggest 400-500 concepts for 156 slots (secondary estimate).
- Masuda's theme was "polarity" (Black vs White, truth vs ideals); Black City and White Forest are the same idea in the world. The box legendaries were built as a matched opposing pair.
- Sugimori's "keep the balance": add something uncool to a too-cool design, something cheerful to a too-somber one. Pokemon should be "ferocious enough" to fight yet endearing enough to want as a companion.
- Type quotas: they had 20 Grass types out of 156 and cut by category, so good designs were dropped for roster balance.
- Masuda asked for more "coolness"; Sugimori used sharper, angular lines.
- Reshiram/Zekrom (Bulbapedia): drafts by Keiko Moritsugu, finals by Sugimori. Zekrom stayed close to the draft; Reshiram changed a lot. Reshiram was made light and graceful against Zekrom's heavier build, even the names were chosen to sound soft (Re) vs hard (Ze). Kyurem is the "empty" third, a wuji/leftover, which fuses with either.

## Reading the evidence

Gen 5 is polarising (TechRaptor). Fans loved the non-legendary "villain-cool" designs built on a strong single concept: Chandelure (haunted candelabra), Volcarona (sun moth), Zoroark (illusion fox), Hydreigon, Haxorus. Legendaries mostly lost to them. Common complaints: quantity over quality, designs derived from objects (Vanilluxe the ice cream, Trubbish, Klink gears, Stunfisk, Alomomola, Bruxish), and a sense of "too busy". The trio of Forces (Tornadus/Thundurus/Landorus) and the Swords of Justice (Cobalion/Terrakion/Virizion) were weakest in every count: sets of near-identical template legendaries diluted each other.

Zekrom/Reshiram worked as a pair on the box: two colours, one silhouette family, one story, mirrored. Victini won the legendary vote with a small, cute, event-mythical build. Kyurem's formal gimmick (absorbing the pair) gave it relevance despite a low favourite share.
