# Generation 7 (Alola) fan reception: notes for ClayPals

Scope: Sun/Moon, Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon, Let's Go (Meltan), regional forms, Ultra Beasts. Research done 23 Nov 2026. The search budget ran out mid-task, so some angles are thin (see "Gaps"). Numbers below are exactly as the sources gave them; estimates are marked.

## Evidence with numbers

1. Nintendo Life Gen 7 poll, 1,906 votes (percent shown on page; counts are our estimate = percent x 1,906):
   Mimikyu 12% (~229, rank 1); Rowlet and Decidueye 9% each (~172); Lycanroc 5%; Incineroar and Primarina 4%; Golisopod and Litten 3%; then a long tail at 1-2% (Bewear, Salazzle, Marshadow, Zeraora, Solgaleo, Lunala, Ribombee, Vikavolt). Trumbeak got 0%. Komala, Crabrawler, Pikipek, Yungoos, Cosmoem all 0.1-0.2%.
   Reading: a single standout (Mimikyu) with the starter lines clustered behind it, and a very long flat tail. Fans concentrate on a few designs.
2. Google/Pokemon "Pokemon of the Year" public vote (reported by Nintendo Life, Feb 2020): Greninja 1st, Lucario 2nd, Mimikyu 3rd (about 99,077 votes per a second report). Mimikyu was the only Gen 7 entry in the top 10 and the top Alola vote-getter. In a 2021 Pokemon Day vote (reported by Digital Trends), Mimikyu again topped Alola.
3. "Every Pokemon is someone's favorite" stats (ComicBook.com, over 257,900 votes total): Mimikyu is the most popular Gen 7 Pokemon; Cosmoem is the least, with 24 people naming it favorite. The author credits Mimikyu's backstory ("equal parts adorable and terrifying") and its wish to be Pikachu.
4. Japanese side: Nintendo Dream's Ultra Sun/Moon ranking of the new Alola Pokemon put Dusk Lycanroc 5th among six (Litten line absent); the 2020 Pokemon of the Year Alola regional results had Rowlet 2nd in region, then Decidueye, Lycanroc, Zeraora, Incineroar (first place cut off in the snippet we saw; Mimikyu was the Alola winner per Nintendo Life).
5. Launch mockery: when the Alolan forms leaked (Kotaku, 2016), the main target was Alolan Persian; Kotaku says many "hate" it or were baffled Game Freak made it, with Garfield-like round face jokes and Photoshops. Ranker's user poll "dumbest new Sun and Moon Pokemon" put Alolan Dugtrio first, with Alolan Persian, Drampa and Oranguru also listed (informal poll, no counts retrieved).
6. Bulbagarden threads (informal, small): a Gen 7 favorites poll gave Mimikyu ~31% (22 votes) over Decidueye (21) and Rowlet (12). Another thread complained about the final starter evolutions (Decidueye, Incineroar, Primarina) as "terrible" while the same starters sit near the top of the polls above: reception is split, not unanimous.

## What designers/analysts say (secondary)

- Game Developer (Rempton Games transcript): Gen 7 details are "an integrated part of the concept" rather than tacked-on spots and spikes; saturated palettes; rounder Alolan forms; nearly every Pokemon has a distinct typing, ability or signature move; the island setting gave a theme (isolation, invasive species, Hawaiian culture). Ultra Beasts deliberately break conventions (no faces, odd materials).
- We could not retrieve any Sugimori/Turner interview for this generation. Do not cite designer quotes from this file.

## Patterns

Loved: Mimikyu (a disguise that is also a story: a lonely ghost wearing a hand-made Pikachu cloth), starters with a strong animal-plus-costume hook (Rowlet's round owl, Incineroar's wrestler), Lycanroc (one form per time of day), Alolan Vulpix (recolour and new type of a loved base).
Mocked or ignored: Alolan Persian (face proportions, flat round shape), Alolan Dugtrio (hair gag), Cosmoem and Trumbeak (little silhouette or personality), Pyukumuku/Komala-style "joke" designs scoring near zero, Ultra Beasts that read as non-creatures.

## Gaps
