# Generation 7 (Alola) signature designs: what fans loved

Scope: Sun/Moon, Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon, Let's Go, Alolan forms, Ultra Beasts. Research was cut short: the shared web search budget ran out, so the dislike side and the Alolan-form and Ultra Beast data are thin. Gaps are marked.

## Evidence with numbers

- Official fan vote (The Pokemon Company, via Google, Feb 2020, 6.6M+ votes). Alola category: Mimikyu 99,077; Rowlet 52,367; Decidueye 44,011; Lycanroc 42,792. Mimikyu also placed 3rd overall (behind Greninja 140,559 and Lucario 102,259), so a Gen 7 newcomer beat nearly every older icon. Source: Bulbapedia "Pokemon of the Year" (summarised through search; the page itself returned 403), Nintendo Life, Anime News Network.
- Nintendo Life Gen 7 poll (1,906 votes): Mimikyu 12%, Rowlet 9%, Decidueye 9%, Lycanroc 5%, Incineroar 4%, Primarina 4%. Starters and Mimikyu dominate, the middle of the pack is flat.
- Pokemon Company International Twitter starter poll (52,630 votes): Rowlet 41%.
- Nintendo Dream (Japan) top six new Pokemon from Ultra Sun/Moon: Alolan Vulpix 4th, Dusk Lycanroc 5th (reported by fan-news sites; I did not open the original).
- Japanese "cutest Pokemon" poll (19,000+ participants, undated): Alolan Vulpix 4th behind Eevee, Sylveon, Glaceon.
- Spanish mini-poll on Alolan forms: Alolan Vulpix 133, Alolan Exeggutor 111, Alolan Sandslash 108, Alolan Sandshrew 49. Small and old (2016), but shows the Vulpix line led the regional forms and the silly Exeggutor still drew votes.
- Editorial: Techraptor's best-of lists Decidueye, Mimikyu, Poipole and Rockruff. Paste called Mimikyu the "voice of a generation".

## Designer intent (Sugimori)

From the Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon Essential Setting Information book (via Siliconera): Sugimori balances designs. A too-cool creature gets something uncool, a too-cute one gets a small imperfection. His example is Oshawott's three cheek freckles, which make the face less cute but more memorable, kept despite requests to remove them. SoraNews24 (2018) reports the same idea: imperfection drives memorability.

## Why the standouts worked

- Mimikyu: a one-line story (a lonely thing wearing a cheap Pikachu rag to be loved) plus a silhouette that is mostly a hand-made costume. The emotional hook is readable without text.
- Decidueye / Rowlet: a cute base that evolves into a cool archetype (ghost archer owl), keeping one carried-over trait (the round body, the hood) so players feel they raised it.
- Lycanroc: one species with two readable personalities (Midday/Midnight, Dusk). Variant forms gave players a choice to own.
- Alolan Vulpix/Ninetales: a known design re-themed by environment (snow in a tropical region). Instantly legible: same shape, new palette and idea.
- Alolan Exeggutor: an extreme, comic scale change (very tall neck) that became a meme. Divisive but unforgettable.

## Disliked (weak evidence)

- Dewpider: Ranker's Gen 7 list (4.2K voters) called it its most divisive pick. No numbers found.
- Alolan forms in general: one Bulbagarden poster called them the generation's weak point; opinion is split. Anecdotal.
- Ultra Beasts, Bruxish, Pyukumuku, Komala, Alolan Exeggutor: I intended to check these and could not; do not rely on them.

## Recurring traits of the loved designs

1. A story or emotion readable from the silhouette alone.
2. A single deliberate imperfection or odd detail.
3. A simple, memorable colour block (few colours, high contrast).
4. A cute-to-cool evolution arc with one visible carried-over element.
5. Variants that change mood, not just colour.
6. A strong theme from the setting (Hawaii: owl archer, wolf, ghost costume).
7. Mid-game creatures meet players often, so repeat exposure builds affection.

## Lessons for ClayPals

- Give every species a one-sentence emotional hook and design the silhouette around it.
- Add one intentional flaw (a mismatched ear, off-centre freckle). In clay this reads as handmade charm.
- Make part-swapped babies keep a hook: define one "signature part" per species that breeds true more often, so mixes stay recognisable.
- Regional variation: same body plan, new biome palette and one reshaped part, a cheap and loved pattern for eleven biomes.
- Offer a two-form branch of a mid-game species so players pick a favourite.
- Rely on starters and one breakout "mascot" per biome; polls show affection concentrates in a few designs, not evenly.
- Do not copy any named creature; use the principles only.
