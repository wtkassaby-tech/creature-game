# Legends Z-A, 2025-2026 designs, legendaries and Megas

Status: THIN EVIDENCE. The shared web-search budget ran out before any search ran, so this note rests on a few Wikipedia pages fetched directly plus general design knowledge (marked "background"). No polls with vote counts were found. Treat the loved/disliked lists as hypotheses to verify.

## What the sources say

Pokemon Legends: Z-A (Wikipedia, fetched 2026-11-23):
- Metacritic 78 (Switch 2) and 70 (Switch). OpenCritic: 66% of critics recommend the base game, 56% recommend the Mega Dimension DLC.
- Sales: over 5.8 million in week one, 1.48 million in Japan in four days, 13.31 million by 30 Sep 2026.
- Criticism centred on flat visuals and a restrictive city setting, not on creature designs. Praise centred on the battle system.
- Mega Evolution returns. Wild Pokemon "Rogue" Mega Evolve and become real-time boss fights. The Z-A story ends with a Mega Zygarde. The Mega Stones for the X/Y starters were gated behind online ranked seasons, and press called this a paywall in effect (GamesRadar headline, as cited by Wikipedia).
- Mega Dimension (10 Dec 2025) centres on Hoopa and gives Raichu two Mega forms. A leak said 16 Pokemon got Megas there.
- Leaked Mega Starmie drew widespread mockery of its legs (Polygon and The Escapist covered it). This is the one clear "disliked design" signal found.

Scarlet and Violet (Wikipedia):
- Metacritic 72/71, 48% of OpenCritic critics recommend. Over 10 million sold in three days, over 28 million by Dec 2025.
- Koraidon and Miraidon are rideable mount legendaries that accompany the player all game, and are revealed as Paradox forms related to Cyclizar. Terapagos is the focus of the Indigo Disk DLC. The page has no reception on them.

Zygarde (Wikipedia):
- Inspired by Jormungandr. Appears as Cores and Cells that combine into a 10% canine form, a 50% serpentine form, and a humanoid Complete form. The Xerneas and Yveltal designs took about 18 months with Atsuko Nishida.
- The form-assembly idea (cells joining into bigger shapes) is the closest thing in the franchise to our part-mixing breeding.

## Background (not verified in this run)
- Box-art legendaries are usually built on a clear silhouette, a two-colour palette, a mythic animal base, and a sense of scale shown through story. Mounts (Koraidon/Miraidon) gain love through shared time with the player, not through stats.
- Mega forms succeed when they keep the base silhouette and exaggerate one signature trait. They fail when proportions break, as the Starmie leg reaction suggests.
