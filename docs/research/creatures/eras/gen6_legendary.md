# Generation 6 (Kalos, ORAS): legendaries, mascots, box-art creatures

Scope: X/Y and Omega Ruby/Alpha Sapphire. Focus on how "special" creatures were designed and which landed. Evidence is from web search (standard mode); gaps are flagged. We take principles only, not any creature.

## Evidence gathered

**Pokemon Company global fan vote ("Pokemon of the Year", 2020):** Greninja (Gen 6 starter) won, followed by Lucario and Mimikyu; Charizard placed fourth with 93,968 votes. A Gen 6 creature winning a worldwide vote years after release is the strongest signal in this set. A 2016 Japanese poll also reportedly named Greninja the most popular Pokemon in Japan (secondary source, Hypebeast/Wikipedia mirror; verify before citing).

**Nintendo Life Gen 6 poll (1,535 votes):** Greninja 15%, Aegislash 8%, Sylveon 7%, Tyrantrum 5%, Hawlucha 5%, Noivern 4%. Box legends scored low: Xerneas 3%, Yveltal 2%, Zygarde 2%, Diancie about 1%, Hoopa 0.7%, Volcanion 0.3%. So in a "favourite of the generation" vote, mascots and mythicals were far behind a starter and a few mid-game creatures.

**Xerneas and Yveltal:** An early IGN poll (reported on Wikipedia, 8 million participants) put Yveltal 2nd and Xerneas 4th among the 69 new reveals, but an official Japanese poll later ranked them 26th and 37th of 721. Hype at reveal was high; long-run affection was moderate. Ken Sugimori told Famitsu he hit "a total wall" designing them, handed the task to Yusuke Ohmura for a while, and took it back; it became a team effort with Atsuko Nishida helping. Press: IGN 2019 ranked Xerneas 10th-best Legendary citing competitive influence; Screen Rant 11th, with Yveltal outside its top 16. Forum opinion splits: some find Xerneas too colourful, others like Yveltal's silhouette, and the tail-claw "took getting used to".

**Zygarde:** The 10% (dog) form drew the most criticism. Smogon's reviewer found the snake/dragon/dog link strange; Bogleech said it "looks like a dog somebody drew kinda funny". The 50% form conveys its cell construction but was called underwhelming; the Complete form was said to deliver the hype (Serebii). Zygarde scored 2% in the Nintendo Life poll. Lesson: a mythic concept (an ecosystem's order made of cells) is hard to express as one readable body.

**Greninja:** Designed by Yusuke Ohmura, finalised by Sugimori, who called it "a bit over the top" but said that works for it and he finds it memorable. Name blends French "grenouille" and "ninja", tying it to Kalos (France) while keeping a clear role fantasy. Reception: top of both fan polls, plus strong competitive and anime exposure (Ash-Greninja).

**Mega Evolution:** The generation's headline mechanic. Search found no per-Mega vote data. A Ranker list criticises Mega Lucario as a "safe" change rather than a dramatic transformation, implying the best Megas change silhouette and attitude, not just add spikes. Treat as qualitative only.

**Designer comment (Sugimori, Nintendo Life 2018):** he wants designs to be as memorable as possible; separate interview coverage says each starter is given a distinct personality.

## Patterns

1. The most loved Gen 6 creature was a *starter* (a creature you raise), not a box legendary. Attachment came from use, story and a strong role fantasy.
2. A one-line concept ("frog ninja") read instantly; abstract concepts (life, destruction, order) read poorly.
3. Strong silhouettes with a single signature feature (scarf-like tongue, sword-and-shield, wings) beat intricate detail.
4. Legendary-ness was signalled through scale, symmetry and colour palette, which gives hype at reveal but weak affection later.
5. Designs that required many forms (Zygarde) lost coherence.
6. Cute-but-cool crossovers (fairy type, Sylveon) performed far above their rarity.
7. Regional flavour (France) gave identity without copying real animals directly.

## Gaps

No data found on Primal Groudon/Kyogre, Mega Rayquaza, Mega Charizard X reception, or Hoopa/Volcanion beyond the poll above.
