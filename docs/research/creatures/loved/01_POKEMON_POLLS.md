Research date: 23 Nov 2026. Purpose: evidence for original creature design. Body-trait counts below are my own coding of the consensus top 40 (a judgement call, shown so it can be challenged). Vote counts are as reported by the cited outlets.

## Surprising winners and why

- **Mimikyu** (3rd globally, 2020): tiny, face never shown, a rag costume of a famous rival. Won on one readable story (a lonely creature in a disguise so it can be loved) and a funny silhouette.
- **Umbreon**: a mostly black fox with glowing yellow rings; top 6 in three polls. One colour trick on a simple body, cool and cute at once.
- **Dedenne, Cinccino, Sableye** (2021 Japan): small, odd or "derpy". They won a vote suspected to be campaign-driven, so read it as proof that a loud community can lift a small design, not as stable taste. Sableye's gem eyes are a very legible hook.
- **Gengar**: round, grinning, simple; 2nd on Reddit 2019, top 10 in 2020 and 2024. Personality in one expression.
- **Flygon**: dragonfly-dragon, 6th in Reddit 2024 and 15th in 2019, with a distinctive mask and wings. A medium design that feels good.
- **Pikachu is not a taste winner**: 19th global, 44th on Reddit, 7th in the 2021 Japan poll. Mascot status is not favourite-design status.
- **Dragonite** (round, friendly, huge) and **Snorlax** (round, lazy): bulk plus a gentle face.

## Strongest patterns

1. Cool or majestic is 68% of the top 40 and cute 33% overlapping; cool wins more often, but cute is a durable second group.
2. Silhouette read beats detail. Almost all have one hook identifiable as a black shape.
3. Canids, felids, foxes and dragons are about half. Familiar animals with a twist win.
4. Upright bipeds (50%) plus sleek quadrupeds (35%) cover most of the list; floating and serpent shapes are rare.
5. Starter and final-form status matters: 11 starter lines and 3 Eeveelutions in 40. A chosen first companion drives love.
6. Legendaries are rare (3 of 40). Players love a partner they raised more than a boss they found.
7. Colour discipline: two or three main colours with one accent, often a dark body with a bright glow.
8. A personality or story hook outperforms design alone (Mimikyu, Absol, Gengar, Sableye).
9. Early generations are nostalgia-boosted (Gen 1 averaged about 6 times a Gen 7 species); a new game must build its own anchors.
10. Odd, small and "derpy" designs can top a poll when fans rally round them, so build in an expressive face and shareable quirks.
