# Loved creatures by early theme: Leaf, Sand, Water, Toxic, plus starters and commons

Research date: 23 Nov 2026. Evidence is fan polls (self-selected, so directional only) and design write-ups. No poll found splits favourites by type, so type lists are built from overall polls filtered by type. Reasons marked "inference" are our reading of the patterns, not a source's claim.

Gen 1 got the most votes and totals fall every generation (a nostalgia effect). [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2019/06/more_than_52000_people_voted_for_their_favourite_pokemon_here_are_the_results)

## 1. Leaf / forest / bug

**Top designs and evidence:** Snivy (#4 Japan 2021, 41,894). Swadloon (#6, 34,204; Japanese Pokemon Center later made Swadloon goods including a wearable hood, [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/news/swadloon-finally-gets-the-respect-it-deserves-with-latest-japanese-pokemon-center-goods)). Bulbasaur (#4 Reddit, #27 Japan). Sprigatito/Meowscarada (top starter of its generation in Japan, three polls). Scolipede and Buzzwole (bug, in the Japan top 30).

**Shared traits (partly inference):**
- Shape: a simple, readable silhouette. Swadloon is a cocoon in a leaf, almost a pure blob; Bulbasaur is a four-legged body plus a bulb. The plant is one clear attachment, not decoration everywhere.
- Face: small, calm, sleepy or smug. Swadloon's sleepy eyes, Snivy's smug look, Sprigatito's cat sass. Personality sits in the expression, not the anatomy.
- Colour: flat greens plus one warm accent (cream, yellow, flower pink). Winning bugs are either round-cute (Swadloon) or sharp and cool (Scolipede, Buzzwole). Plain middle-ground bugs do not show up in any poll.
- Gimmick: the plant is a living part (leaf, bulb, hood, scarf) that can show mood by moving.

## 2. Ground / rock / desert

**Evidence is thin.** Flygon is the only ground entry in the Japan top 30 (#10). Barbaracle (rock/water, #22) shows that a chunky, odd design can place. Garchomp (Dragon/Ground) tied #25 on Reddit. No ground or rock design reaches the Dedenne tier.

**Traits (inference):** the loved ones are either sleek and colourful (Flygon: sand-red body, green eyes, big wings) or a heavy character with a strong face. Boulder-with-eyes rocks are loved as memes and nostalgia, not for design.
- Gimmick: the dune, fossil, burrow or cactus is the animal's body, and a dust or sand move gives it a signature effect.

## 3. Water / coast

**Top designs and evidence:** Oshawott (#9 Japan, 32,191). Piplup (#12 Japan; #1 of 21 in the cutest-starter poll, 20.1%). Empoleon, Greninja and Inteleon (all top 20). Squirtle (#3 cutest; Squirtle Squad sunglasses meme, [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/pokemon/news/all-pokemon-starters-by-generation)). Spheal (#24, a round seal). Mudkip (the "So I herd u liek Mudkips" meme, [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mudkip_Meme); its Nintendo of Europe poll win is uncited). Water is the best-represented type in the Japan top 30 with 7 entries. Quaxly's last place is a warning: a clean, polished design with no strong personality hook lost to a sassy cat and a goofy croc.

**Traits (inference):** round, chubby penguin, seal or otter bodies; a face with attitude (Piplup's proud pose, Oshawott's scalchop stance, Squirtle's sunglasses); blue plus a cream belly and one warm accent. Gimmick: one carried object (shell, scalchop, scarf, fin) that doubles as the weapon.

## 4. Poison / swamp

**Top designs and evidence:** Bulbasaur (grass/poison). Scolipede (#25 Japan). Paldean Wooper and Clodsire: an instant fan favourite and a swift one, memed and drawn for the huge smile and chubby body, and the first Poison/Ground type since Gen 1 ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/paldean-wooper), [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/pokemon-scarlet-and-violets-paldean-wooper-is-the-first-poison-ground-pokemon-since-gen-1)). Gengar (#2 Reddit) and Sableye (#3 Japan) are loved creepy-cute ghosts that sit next to the poison mood. Palworld's Depresso is praised for its grim, sad face ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/palworld/news/cutest-pals-in-palworld-ranked)).

**Traits (inference):** poison is loved when it is a mood, not a danger: a grinning, gloomy or smug face on a purple or teal body, with toxic features that look like toys (bubbles, drips, spots). Realistic slime and rot appear in no poll.

## 5. Starters and the common that people love

**Starters:** winners pair a simple readable body with a face that has personality and a trait you can say in one line. Japan's cutest poll favours the smallest, roundest, most childlike (Piplup, Charmander). Kanto starters win on nostalgia plus memes. Quaxly lost despite polish: the starter must be the creature you want to hug or defend.

**Why some commons are beloved (evidence):**
- Bidoof: a joke about its uselessness (the HM slave in Diamond/Pearl) grew into a god-tier meme, and the company then embraced it with Bidoof Day (1 July) and more Bidoof in Pokemon GO ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/news/pokemon-invents-the-bidoof-roll-for-bidoof-day), [Vice](https://vice.com/en/article/n7bnw7/the-rise-and-fall-of-bidoof-pokemon-snaps-unlikely-hero)).
- Swadloon, Whismur, Cinccino, Dedenne, Spheal: small, round, low-stage creatures that top Japanese polls. Characters with round eyes and faces are easy to get attached to, and cute round characters always seem to rank high in Sanrio votes ([Otakumode](https://otakumode.com/news/560e5e9a67ffbdbd0f4820a8/Why-do-Japanese-Characters-Become-Hits-and-What-Do-They-Have-in-Common)).
- Palworld's Lamball (round pastel sheep, an early pal) and Pengullet (simple, endearing) are the commons people cite ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/palworld/guides/cutest-pals-in-palworld-ranked)).
- Gen 1 commons (Rattata, Zubat) were deliberately generic JRPG enemies, readable at a glance; their love is mostly nostalgia ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/evolution-of-pokemon-designs-gen-1)). Smogon also stresses silhouette: similar outlines are hard to tell apart ([Smogon](https://www.smogon.com/articles/pokemon-art-through-ages)).

**What makes a common lovable, not filler (synthesis):**
1. A silhouette readable in one glance.
2. One oversized friendly trait (smile, ears, tail, leaf).
3. A relatable flaw or mood (sleepy, gloomy, hapless, chubby).
4. Met first and often, so familiarity builds love.
5. A meme-able pose or one-line joke.
6. Clearly different from its neighbours in colour and outline.

## What we should do in each theme (principles, never copies)

**Leaf/forest:** make the plant part a living, emotive organ (perks, droops, closes). Give sleepy or smug faces. Use two greens plus one warm accent. The route common is a round, huggable form with one big leaf feature. Add one sharp, cool bug as a rare.

**Sand/desert-canyon:** the thinnest evidence, so invest here. Give desert creatures strong character faces (grumpy, proud, dozy) and a signature dust effect. Use sand, terracotta and one bright accent so the biome reads colourful, not brown. Rock bodies should be chunky, stackable shapes with a face and a joke.

**Water/coast:** round penguin, seal, otter or crab bodies with a carried object. The starter needs attitude (a stance or prop), since polish alone lost. Blue body, cream belly, one warm accent. Plan a hapless funny common (a shellfish with a worried look) for meme potential.

**Toxic swamp:** poison as mood: a gloomy, grinning or smug face, purple or teal body, toy-like bubbles and drips. A chubby smiling common (the Wooper lesson) beats a menacing one. Avoid gross realism.

**Commons everywhere:** one flaw, one big friendly trait, one clear silhouette. Test: can a child draw it from memory in ten seconds, and does it have one joke? If not, it is filler.

## Gaps and cautions
No type-split poll. No per-species merch or sales data was found. Japan-heavy polls favour round cute designs more than Western or Roblox audiences might, so test cute versus cool in our own playtests. Evidence for Wooloo, Skwovet, Pikipek, Temtem and Coromon was not found. GameSpot and Palworld's own top-10 pages returned 403 and were not read.
