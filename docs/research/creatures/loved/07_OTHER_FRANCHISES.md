# Most-loved creatures in other franchises, and why

Research note, 23 November 2026. Evidence quality is flagged: **official** (publisher poll), **press**, **fan** (forum/wiki/list), **opinion** (editorial). Several searches returned snippets only; gaps are stated, not filled from memory.

## Palworld (strongest evidence, most useful for us)

- **Official polls (Pocketpair).** The 2025 global poll put the human character Zoe first overall; the separate Pal ranking's top three were Chillet, Jetragon and Anubis (per search summaries of [Pocketpair's results post](https://pocketpair.jp/en/news/palworld-global-popularity-poll-the-results-are-in); the post itself only names Zoe, Cremis, Jetragon and Anubis, and the full list of 194 sits on palworld-vote.com, not read). Top Pals got commemorative skins. A 2026 poll of 280+ Pals and characters had Sekhmet first (49,637 votes), with Solenne and Chillet next ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/sekhmet-and-zoe-win-2026-palworld-characters-popularity-poll/); figures not independently checked). Humans took the top three of the earlier all-characters vote ([Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/palworld-developer-asks-which-cute-creatures-fans-like-the-most-but-the-top-3-are-all-humans,1240837/)): players love named personalities as well as creatures.
- **Giveaway vote (semi-official):** Chillet 1st, then Depresso, Katress, Anubis ([Comicbook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/palworld-poll-best-pals), [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/news/pocketpair-announced-the-10-most-popular-pals-in-palworld/)).
- **Chillet** is the most consistent name across polls: a cute dragon-fox hybrid. Anubis (cool, strong, dependable) and Jetragon (a late-game flying mount, aspiration) are the "power" picks. These reasons are my reading of the rankings, not stated by the polls.
- **Depresso** is the clearest meme case. A viral clip showed it mining with an apathetic face; fans called it relatable; the name blends "depression" and "espresso"; the game added a Caffeine Inoculation partner skill to join the joke. It became the subreddit's icon and press asked whether it would be "Palworld's Pikachu" ([KnowYourMeme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/depresso-palworld), [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/palworld-fans-love-depresso-a-miserable-little-guy-who-hates-mining-everyone-and-themselves), [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/will-depresso-become-palworlds-pikachu), [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20240129-26351/)). It is not the most useful Pal, so love came from personality, not stats. Lamball: no evidence found in my searches, so nothing is claimed.

## Digimon

- **Official site poll, April 2020** ([Bounding Into Comics, 13 May 2020](https://boundingintocomics.com/anime/digimon-announces-the-10-most-popular-digimon)): 1 Omegamon, 2 WarGreymon, 3 Gallantmon, 4 Beelzemon, 5 Agumon, 6 Alphamon, 7 Paildramon, 8 Terriermon, 9 Veemon, 10 Patamon.
- Pattern: the top is mostly **evolution finales and fusion forms** (Omegamon is the merge of WarGreymon and MetalGarurumon; Paildramon is a fusion form) plus **partner mascots** (Agumon, Veemon, Patamon, Terriermon). The draw is the arc from small cute rookie to epic form. Fan lists exist on [Ranker](https://www.ranker.com/list/most-iconic-digimon/jay_kobayashi) (763 voters), not read.

## Yo-kai Watch

- No usable official Yo-kai ranking found. Jibanyan (a cat ghost trying to be a hero) is the mascot. The franchise overtook Anpanman in Japanese children's polls in 2015, which ANN credits partly to the catchy anime song and dance ([ANN](https://animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2015-06-29/yo-kai-watch-displaces-long-time-favorite-anpanman-in-children-poll/.89834)). The US counterpart Tomnyan rivalled Jibanyan for "cuteness and energy" ([TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/YokaiWatchProminentYokai), fan source); Pinkipoo, Mermaidyn and Casanono are named fan favourites there. The premise makes every Yo-kai a joke with a job (each one explains an everyday annoyance). The brand later fell in kids' rankings ([SoraNews24](https://soranews24.com/2018/06/25/yo-kai-what-monsters-of-all-kinds-plummet-from-childrens-favorite-character-rankings/)): a mascot alone does not hold a franchise.

## Dragon Quest: Slime

- No formal poll found; evidence is design history and critical opinion. Yuji Horii sketched it and Toriyama finalised it ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2010/07/10/yuji-horii-explains-the-dragon-quest-slimes-origin/)). Toriyama's reported principle: a character needs a feature that identifies it even when drawn small. Teardrop silhouette plus a smirk is one shape and one expression, drawable by a child. It is the first enemy everyone meets, then recurs as the mascot of the spin-offs ([Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=227810), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slime_(Dragon_Quest))).

## Monster Hunter Stories, Temtem, Cassette Beasts

- **MH Stories:** no poll results retrieved. Editorial favourites are Nargacuga (cute, strong, intimidating) and Paolumu ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/which-monstie-did-you-like-best-in-monster-hunter-stories/)). In the main series' Hunter's Choice poll, Zinogre won ([Siliconera](https://siliconera.com/?p=1018003)). Winners have a recognisable personality and signature moves; Stories adds the ride-and-bond fantasy.
- **Temtem:** thin data: a dev Twitter poll on final starter evolutions (Tortenite won) and a statement that Houchic was the most popular starter, then Crystle ([DevTrackers](https://devtrackers.gg/temtem/p/9ace613f-becoming-tortenite-a-quick-look-behind-its-design-process), [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/uk/best-temtem-starters)).
- **Cassette Beasts:** no poll. Press predicted Pombomb as a fan favourite ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/pokemon-fusion-style-rpg-revealed-by-former-stardew-valley-devs/)). The headline feature is **fusion**: any two monsters merge into a new one with combined looks ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Cassette_Beasts)).

## Neopets, Slime Rancher, Moshi Monsters, Skylanders

- **Neopets:** Shoyru reportedly the most common species (about 6% of pets, secondary source), with Kacheek and Kougra high; cuteness explanations are fan-sourced ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/neopets-fan-favorite-ranked/), [Neopets popularity page](https://www.neopets.com/topneopets.phtml)). Recolouring and customisation extend attachment (my inference).
- **Slime Rancher:** no official data. Forum threads favour Tabby ("easily the cutest" in one guide), Honey, Phosphor, and **Largos** (fusions of two slimes) like Tabby-Rock ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/433340/discussions/0/357286119111758068), [GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/listicle/slime-rancher-cuteness-tier-list/)). Each slime is a blob with one feature and one economic job.
- **Moshi Monsters:** a fan-wiki note citing a magazine says Katsuma most popular, then Poppet, Furi, Diavlo, Luvli, Zommer (unverified, [Fandom](https://moshimonsters.fandom.com/wiki/Moshi_Monsters)). Each is a distinct personality on a simple shape.
- **Skylanders:** no large poll. Spyro is the most recognised; a 114-vote 2012 blog poll had Chop Chop first, Spyro third ([blog](https://1ofwiisdom.blogspot.com/2012/02/blog-poll-12-results-who-is-your.html)); TV Tropes says humanoid female Skylanders skew popular with older fans. Weak evidence.

## Shared traits of the winners

1. **A single readable feature or silhouette** (Slime teardrop, Jibanyan, Chillet's fox-dragon, Tabby ears).
2. **A face with an attitude.** Depresso's flat stare, Slime's smirk. Relatable emotion beats generic cute.
3. **A job or a joke**, ideally both: Depresso mines miserably, Yo-kai explain annoyances, slimes feed the farm.
4. **Ugly-cute and unexpected.** Depresso and plain-faced chunky shapes outperform polish.
5. **Evolution and fusion appeal.** Digimon's top four are finales or fusions; Cassette Beasts and Largos build on merging.
6. **Meme potential**: a short quotable personality (name pun, repeated animation), and a game that leans into the joke.
7. **Power fantasy** (Anubis, Jetragon, Omegamon, Zinogre) earns a strong place, but personality wins the memes.
8. **Named partners and characters** count: humans topped Palworld polls; Digimon partners rank beside finales.

## Caveats

Official polls exist only for Palworld and Digimon here; the rest is editorial or fan-forum evidence. Several figures (2026 Palworld votes, Neopets share) come from search summaries not verified at source.
