# 08 Viral creatures and fan art: what spreads, and why

Research note, 23 November 2026. Evidence is graded: **[hard]** = a number or event in a cited source; **[soft]** = a source's interpretation; **[mine]** = my inference. Web search was limited, so several gaps are named at the end instead of filled with guesses.

## 1. Evidence gathered

**Reaction-image creatures (the "mood mascot" pattern)**
- Surprised Pikachu is a still from the 1997 anime. A Tumblr user (Popokko) used it on 26 Sept 2018 for sarcastic shock at an outcome she should have seen coming. By 2019 it was everyday speech ("I was all surprised Pikachu face about it"). Sources credit one instantly readable emotion that fits many situations. [hard + soft] ([Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/entertainment/meme-history-surprised-pikachu), [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/surprised-pikachu))
- Slowpoke became shorthand for "late to the news": a trait (slowness) mapped onto a universal social situation. [soft] ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/pokemon/10-best-pokemon-memes-time))
- Moo Deng (a real baby pygmy hippo, Khao Kheow Open Zoo, Thailand): memes and fan art spread at record speed, and zoo traffic reportedly doubled. One write-up credits her "deranged" expressive face as relatable. Not a game creature, but the clearest recent proof that a chunky body plus one feral expression becomes a mood. [hard + soft] ([Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/memes/moo-deng-baby-hippo/))

**Fan-art volume**
- A 2020 Pixiv tag count (posted by @azuraPKM1542) put the most-tagged Pokemon at Pikachu, Eevee, Lucario, Gardevoir, Snivy, Glaceon, Charizard, Umbreon. Several are customisable or humanoid-readable (Eevee's variants, Gardevoir, Lucario) rather than the strongest. The list is dated and incomplete. [hard, old] ([Anime News Network](https://animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2020-05-13/the-rankings-are-out-for-the-most-popular-pokemon-depicted-in-r-18-art-on-pixiv/.159481)). Pixiv's encyclopedia also tracks bookmark-milestone tags per franchise ([pixiv dic](https://dic.pixiv.net/a/pokemonfanart)); I could not extract per-species counts, and found no AO3 counts.

**Reveal-driven virality**
- Palworld's June 2021 reveal (Indie Live Expo) was framed in the press as "Pokemon with guns". The hook was a four-word absurd pitch; one clip of a Pal used as a bullet shield seeded the "cute creatures, grim world" discourse (NME read it as late-stage capitalism). Reaction split between delight and horror, which drives sharing. [hard] ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/palworld-looks-like-pokemon-with-guns-and-savage-survival-gameplay/1100-6492456/), [NME](https://www.nme.com/features/palworld-a-tale-of-adorable-monsters-friends-late-stage-capitalism-labour-exploitation-2985030))
- After the Jan 2024 launch, individual Pals became memes through behaviour, not stats: Depresso (a gloomy Pal people put to forced labour), Lovander, Cattiva clips. Players made content from what the creature does in the world. [hard] ([Know Your Meme: Palworld](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/palworld), [Depresso](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/depresso-palworld))

**Roblox-specific**
- Italian brainrot: AI-generated hybrid creatures with nonsense Italian voiceovers, e.g. Tralalero Tralala (a shark in sneakers), first posted on TikTok in early January 2025; Tung Tung Tung Sahur (a wooden figure with a bat, Indonesian origin) spread through remixed songs in Sept 2025. One analytics count: about 80k posts and about 3.7 billion views on #italianbrainrot (single source, treat as rough). [hard-ish] ([Wikiwand](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Italian_brainrot), [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/whats-the-tung-tung-tung-sahur-song-and-lyrics-the-steal-a-brainrot-rap-and-brainrot-giga-drill-songs-by-devvey-explained))
- Steal a Brainrot turned those characters into a collectible loop: rarer characters are worth more, and you steal them from others. It hit a record 25.4 million concurrent users (7 Oct 2025; another outlet says 25.2m), the first game past 25m; lifetime visits about 30.1bn vs Grow a Garden's 32.2bn. Roblox as a platform hit about 47 million concurrent users. [hard] ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players), [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/roblox-is-reportedly-bigger-than-the-entirety-of-steam-with-a-whopping-47-4-million-concurrent-users-almost-exclusively-thanks-to-two-games-steal-a-brainrot-and-grow-a-garden)). Grow a Garden peaked at about 21 million in June 2025 ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-peak-concurrent-user-count-hits-record-474m-as-steal-a-brainrot-and-grow-a-garden-compete)).
- Pixiv Encyclopedia has an Italian brainrot entry ([pixiv dic](https://dic.pixiv.net/a/Italianbrainrot)), a sign it reached the fan-art ecosystem, though I have no counts.
- DOORS: fan art is "mostly depicting its paranormal entities" (Seek, Rush, Figure), plus a large animation scene (Jeny_Punker); a Seek figure was teased by Youtooz in April 2023. Fanbase growth across 2022 to 2023. [hard-ish] ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/roblox-doors), [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/youtooz-teases-a-collab-with-doors-showcases-new-seek-figure-roblox))
- Garten of Banban: went viral partly as an ironic meme; negative reviews and glitch compilations became content. Roblox version launched 6 Feb 2023. Claimed 37m TikTok views on the tag (single site). [soft] ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/garten-of-banban))
- Rainbow Friends: no usable data found.

## 2. Traits behind virality (ranked by how well the evidence supports them)

1. **One readable emotion that maps onto a human situation.** Surprised Pikachu, Slowpoke, Moo Deng, Depresso. People post the creature as themselves. [strong]
2. **Absurd, one-line concept.** "Pokemon with guns", "shark in sneakers", "wooden man with a bat". The pitch fits in a caption. [strong]
3. **Cute against cursed.** Palworld's cute Pals in a grim economy; brainrot's eerie AI sheen; Banban's off-putting mascots. Contrast gives people something to argue about, and arguing is sharing. [strong]
4. **A sound or phrase to imitate.** Tung Tung Tung Sahur and Tralalero Tralala are audio-first: the name is the chant. [strong for brainrot, mine elsewhere]
5. **Behaviour people can stage.** Palworld Pals doing chores; DOORS entities with a signature scare. Memes come from what a creature does. [medium]
6. **Easy to draw.** The Pixiv list skews to creatures with a simple silhouette plus a signature feature. Doors entities and brainrot figures are also low-detail. [medium, causation is mine]
7. **Open to remix.** Eevee-style variants and brainrot's template ("animal + object + Italian name") let anyone invent a new one. Remix beats fidelity. [medium]

## 3. How games seeded it

- **Reveal with a hook line, not a feature list.** Palworld's trailer was understood in one phrase and got coverage within hours.
- **Names that sound like a joke or chant.** Short, rhythmic, slightly silly, easy to type and say.
- **Let behaviour leak.** Palworld never marketed Depresso; players found it.
- **Rarity as a status symbol** (Steal a Brainrot's value tiers) gives each creature a "which one did you get" story, shareable by default.
- **Controversy is fuel, but risky.** Brainrot drew accusations over some lyrics, and Banban lived on mockery. Avoid for a kid-heavy platform.

## 4. What this means for our game (mine, for Ayrton to judge)

- Give every species one face that reads a mood at 64px, and show it on the capture and win screens, where screenshots happen.
- Write a one-line pitch and a chantable name per species; test it aloud. Cries should be short and mimicable.
- Aim for a handful of "mascot" species with a signature idle and a signature funny state (the Depresso lesson), not equal polish across all.
- Build silhouettes a child can draw in under a minute; keep part-based breeding legible for remix ("my hybrid" posts).
- Plan a contrast creature per biome (cute but cursed), within Roblox policy and the brief's original-content rule. No imitating brainrot characters or any real IP.
- Seed lore as one-line snippets at reveal, and ship a clip-ready moment per creature before launch.
