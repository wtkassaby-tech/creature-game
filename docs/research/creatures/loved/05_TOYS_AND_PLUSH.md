# Toys and plush: what people pay for (research, 23 Nov 2026)

Purpose: evidence from physical products on which creature designs people love enough to buy, and what that implies for a collect-and-trade creature game in a cozy clay-toy style. Figures come from press and filings found by web search; weak or missing evidence is flagged. Not found in this pass: Pokémon Center / Pokémon Fit sales ranks, Build-A-Bear creature-level sales, Smiski and Sonny Angel unit data (only vague figures), per-product Jellycat sales.

## 1. The numbers

**Sanrio Character Ranking (annual fan vote).** 2025 (40th edition, results 29 June 2025): 1 Pompompurin (up from 4th), 2 Cinnamoroll (about 5.4m votes, ending a five-year streak), 3 Pochacco (3,935,101), 4 Kuromi (3,652,492), 5 Hello Kitty (3,349,642). Record turnout of 63,166,696 votes, up 111% on the prior year. One outlet gives slightly different Cinnamoroll and Pochacco counts; treat those two as approximate. The top five are simple, soft, mostly round, each with one readable trait (Pompompurin's beret, Cinnamoroll's huge ears, Kuromi's jester hood). Dogs and puffs dominate.

**Pop Mart / Labubu (blind-box boom).** 2024 revenue about 13.04bn yuan (about US$1.8bn), roughly +107% year on year. The Monsters line (Labubu's family) about 3bn yuan, about 23% of revenue, up about 727%. CNBC cites about US$423m for Labubu alone. Chase economics: secret/limited figures are commonly cited at 1 in 72 (some say 1 in 144) per box. Retail is about $20 to $30 per blind box; resale for rare variants runs from about $90 to $400 typically, with outliers: an $85-retail skater figure sold for $10,500 on eBay (about 125x), and a human-sized Labubu sold for 1.08m yuan (about $150k) at the first Labubu-only auction. SCMP noted that mainstream resale prices drift back toward retail as supply rises, so premiums concentrate in the rare tail.

**Squishmallows (Jazwares / Berkshire Hathaway).** CNBC: 100 million units sold in one year (price $5 to $30). Later reports claim 400 million units across about 3,000 styles by August 2024 (secondary source, approximate). Reported to drive about 40% of Jazwares' revenue. The brand started with just eight characters (including Cam the Cat and Hans the Hedgehog) and grew by constantly adding named variants.

**Jellycat.** 2024 revenue about £333m (+two-thirds), pre-tax profit £139m; the latest reported year shows sales up 43% to about £450m and pre-tax profit £240m. The Bashful Bunny is the signature product (seven sizes); no unit figures found. Adult "kidults" are credited with the growth.

**Smiski (Dreams Inc.) and Sonny Angel.** Smiski US sales were reportedly 3 to 4 times higher in 2023 than 2022 (no totals). Sonny Angel passed 20 million units by 2019 (Wikipedia, secondary). Both are blind-box lines; Smiski's glow-in-the-dark series is a recurring top seller.

**Funko.** FY2025 net sales $908.2m, down 13.5%, blamed on tariffs and macro conditions. A licence-led vinyl catalogue is not immune to decline; mature catalogues are not crazes.

## 2. Shared design traits of the winners

1. **Baby-schema proportions.** Head about half or more of body height, stubby limbs, short torso, round silhouette. Cinnamoroll, Pompompurin, Squishmallows, Bashful Bunny and Smiski all read as a rounded lump with a head.
2. **Minimal face.** Dot eyes or simple ovals, small mouth or none. Expression comes from pose and one or two features, so people project onto them. Labubu is the exception: a toothy grin gives "cute but mischievous". A small dose of attitude can widen the audience.
3. **One signature feature grown into the body.** Cinnamoroll's ears double as wings and are the whole identity; Pompompurin's beret; Kuromi's hood with horns; Labubu's pointed ears; Smiski's glow. The test is a silhouette recognisable in solid black. For us the feature should be a body part (ears, tail, crest, shell), not a removable accessory, so it survives breeding as a part.
4. **Soft, flat, limited palette.** Pastel or single-hue bodies with one accent colour. Squishmallows and Jellycat sell texture; Smiski sells one flat matte colour. Clay styling can stand in for softness with thumbprints, rounded edges and matte surfaces.
5. **Readable at any size.** Size is a product axis (Bashful Bunny in seven sizes). A creature must read at icon size and in a team of six.
6. **A family, not a hero.** Squishmallows (about 3,000 styles), Smiski (poses), Sonny Angel (headgear), Labubu (series). Variation along one axis (hat, pose, colourway) around a fixed body fuels collecting.

## 3. Blind boxes and chase variants

- Mechanism: a known fixed set, an uncommon "secret" (about 1 in 72 to 144), and a low entry price. Resale premium sits almost entirely in the secret/limited tail; commons trade near retail.
- Hype is cyclical: Labubu resale softened in 2024 before a viral surge, and Funko shows catalogue fatigue. Durable lines (Sanrio, Jellycat) rely on character love, not scarcity alone.
- Scarcity must look special, not just rarer: glow-in-the-dark, a different material, a rainbow or metallic finish, or a costume variant on the same body.

## 4. What this implies for our game

- **Design the silhouette first.** Rounded bodies, big heads, short limbs, one signature body feature per species that passes the black-silhouette test. Faces: dots and a small mouth; give a few species a hint of attitude for contrast.
- **Make the signature feature a breedable part.** It fits part-based breeding: ears, crest or tail are the "Cinnamoroll ears" players hunt and trade. Inheritance of that part should be the headline outcome.
- **Variants along one axis.** Keep a species' body fixed and vary colourway, pattern or finish. Rare finishes (glow, metallic, translucent clay, rainbow) are the chase tier. This matches our rarity tiers and gives trade targets without new models.
- **Common is cheap, rare is special.** Mirror the 1-in-72 style tail, but earned through play only (never paid random, per CLAUDE.md rule 4). Make rarity legible from across a trade screen (glow, sparkle).
- **Names and tiny bios.** Squishmallows' named characters suggest every creature gets a name and a one-line bio; players attach to individuals.
- **A visible roster and a popularity moment.** Sanrio's ranking shows a seasonal vote or featured creature adds social energy cheaply.
- **Test at icon size** and with a plush/figure mock-up render (shape, matte clay, soft shadow).
