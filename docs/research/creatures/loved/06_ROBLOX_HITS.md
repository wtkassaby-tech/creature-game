# 06 Roblox hits: what creature designs win on Roblox

Research date: 23 November 2026. Method: web search plus a few page reads. Evidence quality is mixed and stated per claim. Value lists disagree with each other and are dated; use them for ranking and ratios, not exact numbers. No official poll exists for Sonaria or Loomian Legacy favourites (searched; only tier lists and Tiermaker lists turned up), so claims about "most loved" there are not backed by data.

## 1. Adopt Me! (the trading benchmark)

**Most demanded pets.** Every value list puts the 2019 Halloween-era legacy legendaries on top: Shadow Dragon, Bat Dragon, Frost Dragon. Example figures (value units, one list each, they conflict): Shadow Dragon about 142-154 base, 410 Neon, 1,230-1,380 Mega; Bat Dragon about 100-125 base, 375-400 Neon, 1,135-1,600 Mega; Frost Dragon about 55 base, 150 Neon, 450 Mega; Giraffe 92.5 base, 255 Neon, 815 Mega ([Gamertweak](https://gamertweak.com/adopt-me-pet-value-list/), [Sportskeeda 2021](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/list-roblox-adopt-me-pet-values-december-2021), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/roblox-adopt-me-pets-trading-value-list)). Giraffe is also listed among top pets on a 2026 value site. I found no source with numbers for Owl, Parrot, Evil Unicorn or Crow, so their demand is unverified here.

**Why they are demanded (stated by sources).** Retired eggs and events stop adding supply, so holders keep them and value rises; the community calls legacy legendaries "blue-chip stocks" ([Power Up Gaming, Nov 2026](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/11/05/adopt-me-still-rules-roblox-and-its-pet-economy-is-wilder-than-ever/)). Value is a mix of rarity, availability, demand and potion status ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/adopt-me-trading-values)). The top three are all dragons with dark, ice or night themes and strong silhouettes; the Shadow Dragon was a 1,000 Robux event buy, so scarcity plus a "cool" look.

**Neon and Mega Neon.** Four fully grown copies fuse into a Neon; four Neons fuse into a Mega Neon. Rule of thumb: Neon is roughly 3-5x base, Mega roughly 3-4x Neon. Fly and Ride potions add more ("FR", "MFR"). Design lesson: the same model is re-skinned (glow, then a colour-cycling shimmer) and its value is a visible, earned multiplier. Fusion also removes copies from supply.

## 2. Pet Simulator 99 / X

Pets are tiered up through Huge, Titanic and Gargantuan, and size itself is the rarity signal. Reported top items: Titanic Hippomelon (about 662 billion diamonds RAP, about 82 copies, Oct 2026, [PropelRC](https://www.propelrc.com/?p=134343)); older gem lists put Titanic Hippomelon at 150M gems, Titanic Cat 54M, Titanic Jolly Cat 38.5M ([Mejoress](https://www.mejoress.com/en/pet-simulator-99-value-list-in-roblox/)). Real-money market: Titanic Nightmare Cat $200-300, Huge Santa Paws $65-75, Huge Balloon Cats $40-70, Gargantuan Nyan Cat about $845 ([Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/roblox-en/pet-simulator-99-values/)). Themes: cats (cute, meme-able), holiday and event pets, food hybrids (Hippomelon), internet-culture cameos (Nyan Cat). PropelRC notes limited pets are not automatically the most valuable, so copy count matters more than the "limited" label.

## 3. Grow a Garden pets (2025-26)

Top trade pets: Kitsune (multi-tail fox, top of most lists; Red Kitsune called the "crown jewel", 0.08% Zen Egg origin), Raccoon, Dragonfly, Butterfly ([Gamezebo](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/grow-a-garden-pet-trading-value/), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/grow-a-garden-pets-trading-values-list/)). Value depends on weight, ability, mutations and demand. Abilities (a thief that steals fruit, a pollinator) matter as much as looks, and values drop on quiet servers where the ability has no use. Takeaway: a pet with a visible, useful job trades better than a purely cosmetic one.

## 4. Creatures of Sonaria and Dragon Adventures

Sonaria (Sonar Studios) is the studio's most-played game; its Discord has about 547k members and the wiki lists 350+ creatures in rarity tiers ([Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/creatures-of-sonaria-tier-list/), [Discord listing](https://discord.com/servers/creatures-of-sonaria-714722808009064492)). Existing rankings are combat-strength tier lists, not popularity polls, so "most popular species" cannot be evidenced. Dragon Adventures is Sonar's second title with 1.7B+ visits; its trophy creatures are limited ones (Guild War dragons such as Moixaura, Constello, Geoteryx) and top tier-list dragons (Mother Dragon, Chronocus, Venid, Volkumos) ([VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/guides/dragon-adventures-tier-list), [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/guild-dragons-roblox-dragon-adventures)). Pattern again: dragons plus event-exclusive scarcity.

## 5. Loomian Legacy and Bee Swarm

Loomian Legacy (the closest Pokemon-like Roblox game) has only Tiermaker lists and starter rankings; its PvP tier list names Stratusoar at the top ([Gamer Empire](https://gamerempire.net/loomian-legacy-pvp-tier-list/), [Tiermaker](https://tiermaker.com/list/roblox-games/july-2023-loomian-legacy-15928916/3180671)). No hard favourites data. Bee Swarm Simulator: 3.6B+ visits, 95% rating; the plain Basic Bee is the mascot and one of only three bees with an official plush (with Tabby and Vicious Bee). Gifted bees get a golden outline and rank higher ([Fandom wiki](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Bee_Swarm_Simulator), [Pro Game Guides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/bee-swarm-simulator-bees-tier-list-all-bees-ranked/)). Lesson: a simple, round, instantly readable mascot silhouette carries a brand. Doors and Rainbow Friends searches returned nothing useful; no evidence collected.

## 6. 2025-26 hits

Steal a Brainrot hit a record 25.4M concurrent users in October 2025 and has passed 68.7B visits ([Pocketgamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players), [Cubix](https://www.cubix.co/blog/top-10-most-popular-roblox-games/)). Its creatures are absurd meme characters with tiered rarity and mutations, and it spawned Catch a Brainrot, a Pokemon-style catch-and-flex game ([Games.gg](https://games.gg/news/catch-a-brainrot-roblox-pokemon/)). Press now frames creature collecting as a Roblox growth genre ([European Gaming](https://europeangaming.eu/portal/latest-news/2025/10/17/194150/are-creature-collecting-games-the-future-of-roblox/); page blocked, headline only). Meme recognition, not craft, drove this wave.

## 7. What wins on Roblox (synthesis)

Evidence-backed:
- **Dragons dominate the top of trade lists** (Adopt Me top three, Dragon Adventures trophies). Cool-leaning dark or ice dragons beat cute ones at the very top.
- **Scarcity beats design.** Retired supply, fusion and tiny drop rates (0.08%) set value; the look must only be strong enough to display.
- **Variants as visible multipliers.** Neon/Mega, Gifted outline, Huge/Titanic size, mutations. Each is the same model with a clear, readable change (glow, cycling colour, scale, outline), so rarity reads from across a room.
- **Utility plus looks.** Grow a Garden abilities and Bee Swarm stats tie value to function.
- **Cute and cool both sell.** Cats, foxes and bees are cute and trade at the top of their games; dragons are the "cool" apex. Meme humour is a third lane for the youngest players.

Inference (my reading, not directly sourced): the audience is roughly 9-15, so silhouette clarity at thumbnail size, bright saturated colour, and a "flex" moment (glow, aura, big scale) matter more than fine anatomical detail.

## 8. Implications for our game (all [Default], for Ayrton to review)
1. Keep a dragon family as a chase line, with dark, ice and fire elemental variants.
2. Make rarity visible: a glow or aura tier, a size tier, and an outline for "gifted" variants, all as data on the species, not new models.
3. Use breeding generations as the fusion-style sink, so supply shrinks as value grows.
4. Give the starter line a simple round mascot silhouette.
5. Policy still applies: variants come from earned play, never paid random outcomes; any Robux-bought random item shows odds first.
