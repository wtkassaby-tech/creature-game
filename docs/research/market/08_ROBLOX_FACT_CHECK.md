# Roblox fact check: claims in reports 01, 02, 03, 04, 06 and BRIEF section 11

Date: 16 November 2026 (pages fetched 15 November 2026 server time; the docs pages carry "last_updated" 14 November 2026 or earlier)
Status: research, nothing decided

## Summary

- Checked at source on create.roblox.com (docs as markdown), devforum.roblox.com (JSON topics) and about.roblox.com (Community Standards, newsroom). Every row below was opened, not taken from a snippet. Other hosts stay blocked and were not tried.
- Most money and policy claims are CONFIRMED: paid random items rules, both PolicyService fields, 70% share, the DevEx rates, Plus ($4.99, 30 April 2026), subscriptions, regional pricing bounds, cross-game sales ending 29 May 2026.
- Two claims change recommendations. (1) The Community Standards ban "simulated and actual gambling", wider than the brief's "real money, Robux or items exchanged" wording. (2) The U.S. 18+ DevEx rate (0.0054) says games with custom nonhuman characters or no visible player character qualify, so the "unverified" caveat in report 01 can mostly be dropped (R15-only avatar setting and a compliance check still apply).
- BRIEF section 11 is right on the headline facts (16+ route has no fee, no 2FA, no evaluation; all-ages route has ID check, 2FA, 1,000 Robux fee or two months of Plus or Premium, 250 highly engaged plays in 60 days). It omits three conditions on the 16+ route (age check by selfie estimate or ID, account at least 2 days old, one content-rating question) and says "Government ID check" where Roblox accepts facial age estimation for under-18s.
- Wrong or outdated in the reports: the 30-day escrow (it is for Creator Store sales; in-game passes and products hold about 5 days), the "6-second write limit" (now per-key throughput of 4 MB a minute), the "under 1,000 draw calls and 1,000,000 triangles" example (not on the performance pages), and "developer products opt in one by one" (new items are enrolled in Managed Pricing by default).
- Not checkable here (host blocked): ProfileStore docs, Fandom, Rolimons, news outlets, Lyra, third-party guides.

Verdict key: CONFIRMED, WRONG (correct fact given), PARTLY (true but incomplete or stale), NOT FOUND (not on the official page it was attributed to).

## 5. Corrections that change a recommendation

1. **Gambling wording (BRIEF 11, DECISIONS: coin bets removed).** The ban is on "simulated and actual gambling" and on "betting, wagering, or casino-style mechanics", whatever is staked. The removal of coin bets was the right call. Also review any "wager" or "ante" mechanic, loot-box animation styled as a slot machine, or wheel spin in the game against this wider wording, even when only free coins are involved. Update the brief's gambling paragraph to quote the Community Standards text.
2. **U.S. 18+ DevEx rate is probably available (report 01, "do not plan revenue on it").** A turn-based creature game whose player has no visible humanoid avatar, or a custom nonhuman character, meets the written criteria. The conditions are R15-only avatar settings (or a custom non-R6 system), 100% of active playtime, and a compliance check; only U.S. players aged 18+ who are age-verified count; passes, products, subscriptions and private servers count. The 16+ soft launch fits this. Still do not bank on it, but remove "unverified" from the plan and set Avatar Settings accordingly.
3. **BRIEF 11 publishing table.** Rewrite the 16+ row to: age verified by facial age estimation or government ID, account in good standing and at least 2 days old, one content-rating question; no fee, no 2FA, no evaluation. Rewrite the all-ages row to: age verification (facial estimation under 18, government ID for 18+), 2FA, publishing fee of 1,000 Robux or two consecutive months of Plus or Premium, then 250 unique plays by highly engaged age-checked users within 60 days; fee refunded 90 days after eligibility. The checklist line "Start Roblox Plus ... since it needs two months to count" is correct.
4. **Hold times (report 01, `ROBLOX_POLICY.md`).** In-game passes and products are held about 5 days, not 30. Only Creator Store sales, local-currency subscriptions (30 days), Creator Rewards and Plus sign-up earnings (60 days) are longer. Cash-flow planning for the first payout should use 5 days.
5. **Performance budget (report 03).** The "Roblox example of 1,000 draw calls and 1,000,000 triangles" is not on the official pages. Describe the 500 draw call and 300,000 triangle budget as the project's own, set from a measured baseline device. Adopt the confirmed streaming settings: `StreamOutBehavior = Opportunistic`, `StreamingIntegrityMode = PauseOutsideLoadedArea`, `ModelStreamingBehavior = Improved`, leave min and target radius at 64 and 1024.
6. **Trading and data (report 06).** Drop the 6-second write limit as a reason for the trade cooldown; use per-key throughput (4 MB a minute write) and the official advice to keep atomic changes in one key. Roblox's own player-data sample uses an `UpdateAsync` metadata lock with a 180-second save interval; the two-phase trade design still stands. Keep "nothing bought with Gems or Robux is tradable": it satisfies `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` without per-user branching, and a DevForum community answer (not official) says indirect payment counts.
7. **Managed Pricing.** New items are enrolled by default, so the shop UI must read prices at runtime from the start (no hard-coded Robux numbers), and new developer products start as Not Listed.
8. **Tournaments and prizes.** Prizes must stay in-game and cosmetic; real-money or Robux prizes need Roblox's involvement. This supports the brief's "prizes are created by the game" line.
9. **Fee refund text conflicts across three Roblox pages (25, 100 players, or 90 days).** Do not promise a refund condition to Ayrton; say "refundable" and check the Creator Hub payment dialog at the time.
