== Biome-reached rule: what counts as a creature's biome? ==
  biome-1 player, all parts from biome 1: best total 460.5 (Burrowbear/Stumpback/Burrowbear/Mossbun/Mossbun)
  if only the torso's biome is checked: 475.5 (Scuttlejaw/Stumpback/Scuttlejaw/Mossbun/Mossbun), +3%
  breed cost of that hybrid if tier comes from the torso: 1000 vs from its latest part: 2000 (x2)

== Level loophole: traded high-level parents ==
  level 60 creature vs Greenwood giant (level 12): stat multiple x3.18
  level 24 creature vs Greenwood giant (level 12): stat multiple x1.55
  their bred child is level 24; passes the biome rule because every part is from Greenwood
  with a badge level cap (0 badges -> 15): both fight as level 15, x1.14 vs the giant

== Rare colour onto a strong line (recessive gene, kin rule on) ==
  common/uncommon rare-colour parent, limit 5: rare colour shown on a strong-line child within 5+12 breeds in 97% of runs; median breeds 8
  gem-tier parent, limit 3: rare colour shown on a strong-line child within 3+12 breeds in 97% of runs; median breeds 6
  Ruby parent, limit 2: rare colour shown on a strong-line child within 2+12 breeds in 97% of runs; median breeds 5.0
  limit 1: rare colour shown on a strong-line child within 1+12 breeds in 72% of runs; median breeds 7
  without the kin rule a carrier could breed straight back to its rr parent: 50% per breed
