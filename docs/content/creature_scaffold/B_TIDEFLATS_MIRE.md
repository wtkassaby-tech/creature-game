- **Meshy image prompt** (reference only: over Meshy's 800-character limit; paste the short prompt): the full six-block prompt (STYLE LINE, the plan's STANDARD BODY LINE word for word, the species look part by part, SEPARATION LINE, COLOUR LINE, VIEW LINE, then "No text, no base."). Paste it whole into Nano Banana Pro or GPT Image (1:1, at least 1024 px), ideally with two approved creatures attached as style references. It is too long for Meshy's own text-to-image box.
- **Short prompt**: for Meshy's text-to-image box (800 characters at most). It opens with the shared style line (see the art style note at the top of the file), then a compressed pose line for the plan, the species in one breath, and "No text, no base." - **3D settings**: Meshy image-to-3D settings from `04_PROMPT_CRAFT.md` and the triangle budgets from `00_BREEDING_STANDARD.md` section 6, with what to watch for on this creature.
- Neither prompt ever names the species (Meshy writes the name out as a plaque).
- Every creature uses the same eye wording ("big glossy cartoon eyes with white highlights"), so the roster reads as one family; brows, lids and mouth carry the personality.
- Two-legged creatures: Meshy Pose = A-Pose. Four-legged and giants: Pose off.
- Every model is mirrored about its centre line on import, so nothing in these designs is lopsided; where the personality is lopsided (Cocklemole's crooked helmet, Moatkeep's crooked tower), the lopsidedness moves into the animation.

## Biome design notes

**Tide Flats (sea teal, wet-sand cream, coral pink, barnacle grey).** Every Tide Flats creature carries one thing from the shore: a shell worn or grown, foam, a fin, barnacles. The six Water species read as wet and smooth (pups, fish, toads, a seal face); the two Sand species read as built from sand (scoop claws, crenellations, a sand cast) and both wear a shell or a castle on the head, so the Sand pair look like cousins. Coral pink is the biome's shared accent (tongues, ribs, anemone hands, crest). Ugly-cute slot: Gulpmaw.

**The Mire (olive, peat brown, moss green, bruise violet, toxic lime as accent only).** The Toxic species carry warning colours (sulphur yellow, tangerine, lime) rather than an all-purple body, so the Mire does not look like other games' purple poison types; bruise violet stays a small accent (Fenwhisk's paws). The two Water species, Mistrel and Lilyshade, share a frost-blue or frost-white tip motif that tells players they carry Hoarmist (Frost). Lumpy, drooping, swollen shapes: the peat mound, the water-balloon tail, the pot belly, the sagging water bag. Spooky-cute slot: Snaggar.

**Breeding stat parts.** Tide Flats torsos carry 55% of Defence (shells, barnacles, castles, plates); every Tide Flats torso here has a visible "armour" feature so the Defence part looks like what it does. Mire tails carry 13% of Hp; every Mire tail here is swollen or heavy (balloon, bag, bulrush, barbed club, paddle) so the Hp part looks like what it does.

## Tide Flats

### Glissot

*Breathless tide-line skimmer that cannot stand still. Water, Quad, Common, S (45), sprinter.*

- **Description:** A sleek little sea-hound pup that races the tide line with its tongue out, a curl of white sea foam standing up on its forehead like a breaking wave. It jogs on the spot whenever it has to wait.
- **Intended appeal:** Pure puppy energy that kids recognise at once: the happy pant and the jog-on-the-spot idle are the personality, and the wave curl on its brow is the one thing anyone can draw from memory. Sleek, simple surfaces make it the Tide Flats creature that looks best at a glance in a trade window. Breeders want its big webbed feet (the plan's speed part). Rare look to chase: Pearl Streak.
