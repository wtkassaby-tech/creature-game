## 1. How to use this file

- **Breedable species** (the 21 catchable creatures) use the standard's six blocks in order, pasted in full: STYLE LINE, the plan's STANDARD BODY LINE word for word, the species look part by part (head, body, front limbs or arms or wings or fins, legs, tail), SEPARATION LINE, COLOUR LINE, VIEW LINE, then "No text, no base." Paste the whole prompt; only the species look differs between creatures.
- **Full prompts are reference only** (over Meshy's 800-character limit): paste the short prompt into Meshy. Art style (owner decision, 24 November 2026): clay bodies with cartoon eyes; every short prompt opens with the style line "Claymation creature for a Pokémon-inspired Roblox game: matte hand-sculpted plasticine with thumbprint texture, chunky rounded shape, big glossy cartoon eyes with white highlights, expressive face.".
- **Short prompts** fit Meshy's 800-character box: the shared style line (matte clay body, big glossy cartoon eyes with white highlights), a compressed body cue for the plan, the look, and the closing line. Each shows its length.
- **Giants and mythics** use free-format prompts. Each says where it keeps or breaks the plan proportions, and why.
- Per creature, the five lines asked for (description, appeal, prompt, short prompt, 3D settings). The roster plan's other template items (element, plan, rarity, size, role, three signature parts, personality, journal facts, moves, breeding hook, originality) are in the tables at the top of each biome, so the creature blocks stay short.
- **Meshy (Ayrton regenerates every creature on a paid plan):** for every breedable species, `04_PROMPT_CRAFT.md` MESHY SETTINGS, "Common to Diamond" column (about 3,000 triangles, texture on, matte, PBR off, Image Enhancement off for an approved concept, Refine Prompt off), with **part segmentation on** so the head, body, limbs and tail come out as separate parts. The paid plan allows Meshy 7.1 High Detail with multi-view: use it (front, side, back, three-quarter turnaround) wherever the back matters (Orrerback's dome, Halcyre's wings, Mossaltar's altar, every serpent's tail, every giant and mythic), then decimate each part to the budget. Giants: the "Giant" column. Mythics: see each block.
- **Look-alikes are flagged, never designed away** (Ayrton, 23 November): every design here is the one we think is best. Where it resembles another franchise's creature, the originality table says what it resembles, how closely, and a fallback idea, and section 6 (the end of the file) lists every flag in one place for Ayrton to judge.
- Glow is always "painted bright, not glowing" in the prompt, and becomes Neon, a light or particles in Roblox. Glass is "opaque coloured panels with dark outlines". Nothing is see-through, nothing floats loose, nothing is thinner than a finger.

## 2. The Abyss (biome 10)

Town "Starfold", 5v5, Diamond tier starts here ([Locked]), the last rival battle ([Locked]). Plan 13: the Starwell Tunnel opens under the one night sky in the world; black star-flecked sand, the Starwell (a ring of standing stones round a well of light), the tear hanging over the Rift to the east, and the Crown seen from inside.

**Look:** midnight navy, black sand, starlight cream points, nebula pink and teal, gold for constellation thread (painted, the patchwork theme at its most cosmic). Sleek, ringed, streaming shapes; every Abyss tail is a streamer, a comet or a ring, because **tails are the Abyss breeding-stat part (Speed)**.

**Types:** Cosmic 8 (Orrerback, Cometkit, Halcyre, Spyglint, Cometfinch, Nebulark, Galaxeel, Portaloop), Arcane 2 (Hourling, Ouroring, each with a Crystal move on the torso that beats Cosmic back), Fire 1 (Bolide, with a Storm move on the torso). Cosmic beats Fire and Arcane, so those three are the underdogs, each carrying its answer on the torso as the roster's proven shape requires.

**Status idea:** Drift (cannot switch out for two turns), a fixed effect with no chance, on Galaxeel's, Ouroring's and Starfathom's moves. Needs Ayrton's yes as a battle rule.

**Gifts (proposal):** quest creature Halcyre (it leads the player to the Last Bridge); token Spyglint (as the roster plan); the rival's gift (his own starter, [Locked]) after the last rival battle, here in Starfold. **Diamond showcase:** Orrerback (its dome is the broadest surface in the biome for the sparkle and sheen). Halcyre is the second showcase.

## 4. The mythic tier

Mythics are not a rarity tier (roster plan section 5): rarity belongs to individuals, and the cosmic wrap stays the only way to Cosmic rarity ([Locked]). A mythic is a species with a flag (proposed `mythic = true`): one per player, bound to the account, numbered in the order players earn it, gifted by the story, never a wild spawn and never sold. Whether a mythic can breed is Ayrton's call.

**The lore in one paragraph (one connected proposal, keeping every [Locked] beat).** The world was stitched together by the **Firstling**, the first creature, whose glass panes are where every creature's parts come from (which is why parts can be swapped at all). It sleeps in the Hollow holding the world's seams shut. The mentor, chasing the perfect bloodline, used a fallen star fragment in a breeding experiment; the fragment was a chip of the Firstling's glass, and using it tore the sky. Through the tear came the **Unraveller**, which pulls seams apart. The experiment's only result is the **Frayling**, small, frightened and half-finished, which the mentor has hidden ever since. The rival, after his first loss, swallows a black thread he finds near Starfall Crater: **Blackstitch**, a splinter of the Unraveller, which leaves him partly controlled. **Nimbram**, the ancient ram whose cloud fleece is the halo on the Crown, has been holding the tear small; its fleece greys each chapter. When the player destroys the Unraveller, the Firstling wakes, mends the sky, and joins the player with the cosmic wrap.

**The Unraveller's 6v6 team (proposal).** Five "unravelled" creatures, then the Unraveller itself. An unravelled creature is a normal creature drawn by the Assembler with its parts pulled a little out of their sockets and joined by dark thread (Beams between the existing attachments) and a dark tint; one of its parts is "lost", so that part's moves are locked. This costs no new meshes and shows the threat in the game's own language: it undoes what breeding builds. Proposed five: an unravelled Hollowbough (the first giant), an unravelled form of the player's own starter species, and three from the biomes the player cleared last (Stormreach, the Arcane biome, the Abyss), so the fight looks back over the journey. The Unraveller's own phases: Unpick (each turn it locks one of your active creature's parts), Tear (at half health, Drift on your whole side), and Last Thread (low health; any Crystal hit, the Firstling's glass, stitches one of its phases shut). All fixed effects, no chance, as the battle rules require.
