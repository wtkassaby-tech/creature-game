## 2. Winged plan conventions

These extend `00_BREEDING_STANDARD.md` section 1.3 (bands, sockets, budget) and do not repeat it. They apply to every Winged species in every biome (Emberfall, Stormreach, Arcane, Abyss, the Hollow) and to Squallray's giant form. Ayrton has not yet confirmed the plan (roster plan decision 4); until then these are the proposal.

### 2a. The body in one picture

A round egg held level, a big head at the front on a short neck in a collar, two wings folded straight down the sides like closed fans, two short legs under the middle, and a short tail fan straight back. It stands like a plump perching bird on a shelf. The same six joints and five templates as Quad and Biped: the **wing is the `FrontLimb` template**, one left-side mesh mirrored for the right.

### 2b. Wings

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **One closed fan, thick and rounded.** Feathers are drawn as 3 to 5 rounded scallops or lobes along the lower edge and as painted rows, never as separate quills. Bat wings are a leathery paddle with 2 or 3 soft lobes and painted veins. Fin-wings (Squallray) are a thick rounded fin. | Meshy breaks thin membranes and single feathers (04, section 6); a closed fan segments cleanly |
| **Thickness:** at least as thick as a finger on the toy, about 0.08 L at the root tapering to no less than 0.04 L at the tip. | Survives decimation; reads at phone size |
| **Rest pose: folded straight down along the side**, flat in the YZ plane, tip toward -Y and slightly +Z, **a small clear gap between wing and body** in the concept. | Standard 1.3; the gap lets Meshy split the wing. This **supersedes 04's Winged pose line** (wings spread in a V): spread wings do not fit the shared rest pose |
| **The same on both faces.** Any pattern (veins, stars, hail rows, gold trim) is painted identically on the outer and inner face, and is symmetric about the wing's own mid-plane. | One mesh serves both sides, and animation opens the wing by rotating it about Z |
| **Wing root is a plain coat-coloured ball** at the shoulder socket; patterns start at least 1 R below it. | Joint zone rule; the colour step falls at the wing root |
| **Length 0.70 to 1.10 L folded; tip at least 0.10 L above the ground** with the legs at their shortest in the band. | A long wing on short legs must not drag after breeding |
| **Wings own:** feathers, scallops, veins, wing spurs (a blunt thumb knob at the wrist), painted trim. **Never:** anything that reaches onto the tail or over the back. | Standard 2.2 |

### 2c. Head, body, legs, tail

- **Head:** beaks, crests, combs, wattles, ear tufts and antennae are head features. A swept-back crest ends before the collar and never lies on the back. Beaks are short and chunky (no long thin bills); a hooked beak is fine.
- **Body:** belly features (Boomthroat's pouch, a breast band) sit below the collar and above the leg sockets. Back features (Squallray's cloud tufts) stay inside the back's outline, at least 1 R behind the collar and 1 R in front of the tail, and never sit where the folded wing touches the side.
- **Legs:** two, under the middle of the body, straight down, feet flat. Big feet (Gustlet's webbed feet, talons) are fine; they sit under the plug, not splayed sideways. Length 0.30 to 0.50 L.
- **Tail:** a short fan, fork, vane or tuft pointing straight back, 0.25 to 0.60 L, never touching the ground or curling over the back. A tail may carry a signature object (Vanecrest's weathervane arrow) if it stays in that band.

### 2d. Motion (for the Animation step)

- **Idle:** standing on two legs; a small wing shuffle (each wing rotates out 10 to 15 degrees about Z and back).
- **Flap and glide:** both wings rotate about Z at the plug to about 80 degrees open; glide holds them level. Because the mesh is symmetric about its own plane, the mirrored wing looks right open or closed.
- **Battle:** Winged creatures stand on the field like the others, and lift into a short hover only for their attack animation, so the battle layout and the camera need no change.
- **Overworld:** a hop-and-flap over short gaps; real flight is not a feature (no new movement rules).

### 2e. Mixing and stats

- Winged parts mix only with Winged parts (one rig per plan). With the bands agreeing, any wing looks right on any Winged body; Squallray's giant form breeds only if Giant Candy allows it (DECISIONS, 21 November).
- **Suggested part roles:** wings carry Speed in Stormreach and Attack (wing spurs) in Emberfall; legs carry less Speed than on Quad or Biped (fliers are not runners), so a Winged creature's Speed lives mostly in its wings. The roster gate sets the numbers.
- Every Winged species shows a signature feature on three parts, and the wings are usually one of them, so a baby's wings show where it came from.

### 2f. Checklist additions (on top of standard section 5)

- [ ] W1 Wings folded straight down, a gap from the body, tips above the ground.
- [ ] W2 The wing looks the same from both faces; nothing thinner than a finger.
- [ ] W3 Crest, beak and tail stay inside their own parts; nothing lies on the back.
- [ ] W4 Two legs under the middle, feet flat; the body is level.
- [ ] W5 Budget: wings 300 to 450 each, legs and tail 200 to 300, whole creature at or under 3,600.
