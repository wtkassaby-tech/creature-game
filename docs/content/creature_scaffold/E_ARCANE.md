## 1. The biome in one page

**Where and what.** Lanternmoor is a purple cave valley in the Western Highlands at height 100, reached down the Cloud Stair from Stormreach: "the longest descent in the game", from high noon to violet twilight under the cave arch, wind giving way to a music-box tone and owls (plan 13). Places that host creatures: the cave valley and Lanternmoor's lanes, the Lantern Spire (a crooked spire whose purple light blinks a slow pattern, visible from Greenwood since day one), Toadstool Dell (giant glowing mushrooms, a troll's footbridge, fairy rings), Mirror Mere (still lake, willow island, the old tamer's empty cottage) and Starfall Crater (opens in this chapter, when the mentor's secret comes out; DECISIONS, 23 November). The Old Lane leads from here home to Greenwood's old fence: the nostalgia loop.

**The feeling (bible 09).** An old fairy-tale book left open in a cave at dusk, handmade from clay: warm lantern light inside a cool violet world, so it is cosy, not gloomy. Crooked, pointed, leaning storybook shapes on heavy round toy bases. Violet or indigo is a main colour on at most six of the ten species (here: four), with moss, cream, pebble grey and paper carrying the rest.

**Battle structure.**
- Native type **Arcane** (beats Frost and Storm; weak to Toxic and Cosmic, `src/shared/TypeChart.luau`). Seven Arcane species, two Storm, one Frost, as the roster plan sets.
- **Underdogs:** Rimefawn (Frost) and Stringlet (Storm), each with a torso-only **Cosmic** move that beats Arcane back and teases the Abyss.
- **Breeding hook of the biome: move-rich tails.** Every Arcane-biome tail brings **two** moves where other tails bring one (roster plan proposal). Here it is made readable: **on every native Arcane species, one of the two tail moves carries Hex** (the proposed Arcane status: locks the target's last-used move for two turns, no chance). So "want Hex? take an Arcane tail" is a rule a 10-year-old can learn, and it travels across biomes in breeding. The underdogs' tails bring two moves without Hex.
- **Gifts:** quest creature Underbridge (Uncommon) unless the Frayling's quest takes that slot (agent F); token Sparkowl (Storm beats Cosmic, the next biome).
- **Echo of home:** Jackalorn sits beside Gorsling, Capgnome beside Stumpwarden, Rimefawn beside Sloshcalf: Greenwood shapes grown strange (bible 09).

**Rare-look rule (bible 09, "the lantern lights up").** Each species' rare look lives on the one part that owns its signature feature (standard section 3.5): that feature's accent swaps to **Starlight silver-white #E8EEFF**, with brighter glaze and silver rune marks. Body colours stay, so the species is recognised at once. How rare looks are earned is still Ayrton's call.

**Personality pairs** were checked against `docs/content/CREATURE_PERSONALITY.md`: no temperament-and-gesture pair repeats one already used (other new biomes were not written yet, so the lead should re-check across C, D and F).

## 3. Species

### Jackalorn
*Look and prompts: draft, to redo against Ayrton's references.*

- **Description:** The horned hare of old tales: a quick, sly lilac hare with short forked antlers tipped in moss, darting along Lanternmoor's hedges and the Old Lane like a Greenwood runner that wandered into a storybook.
- **Intended appeal:** A cool, fast silhouette (ears plus antlers) that still has a buck-toothed grin; the folklore jackalope is instantly "a magic animal" to every age, and its antlers are a head part players will want on every Quad they breed.
- **Meshy image prompt:**

> cozy handmade clay toy creature, soft matte plasticine, chunky rounded proportions, big friendly eyes, original creature design, no clothing, no held objects, full body. a chunky four-legged creature with a rounded loaf-shaped body about one and a half times as long as it is tall, back level, standing square on four short straight legs set under the four corners of the body with a clear gap between the legs, front and back legs the same length, legs about two-thirds as long as the body is tall, feet flat on the ground; a big round head a little less than half as long as the body, held forward at the height of the back on a short thick neck that rises out of a soft rounded collar at the front of the body; a tail that starts as a small round stub at the top of the rump and points straight back; left and right sides mirror-symmetric. Head: a round hare head with sly half-closed eyes, a small pink button nose and a buck-toothed grin, two long upright ears with soft rounded tips, and between the ears a pair of short chunky forked antlers like little branches, bark brown and as thick as fingers, each antler tip wrapped in a small round tuft of moss-green clay with one tiny lantern-gold mushroom on each tuft. Body: a compact rounded dusky twilight-violet body with a moonmilk-cream belly and a few small cream star-shaped freckles kept in the middle of the back. Front legs and back legs: short sturdy legs with big round mitten feet, the back feet a little larger than the front, soles painted cream. Tail: a short stub ending in a round fluffy pompom, moonmilk cream with one small painted moss-green leaf. each body part is a separate, clearly shaped piece: the head, the body, each leg and the tail keep their own features; nothing hangs over, wraps around or runs across from one part onto another; where the head, legs and tail meet the body, the surface is plain main colour; no clothing, straps, chains or props. simple flat colour blocks: one solid main colour over the whole creature, the same on the head, body, legs and tail; one lighter belly colour; small details such as eyes, mouth, horns, claws and hooves in clearly different colours; no gradients, no painted shadows or highlights, no stripes or spots running across the joints. plain white background, even soft lighting, the whole body in view, three-quarter view from the front left, standing still in a neutral pose. no text, no base.

- **Short prompt (under 800 characters):**

Four short straight legs of equal length under the body corners, back level, head forward on a short neck in a soft collar, tail straight back, symmetric. - **3D settings:**
  - Body plan: Quad.
  - Triangle target: 3,000 (hard 3,600); Head 600-900 (the antlers take a share), Torso 600-900, FrontLimb, Leg and Tail 200-350 each.
  - Notes: Meshy Smart Topology (T2.5), pose off, texture on, matte, PBR off. Antlers are the thin-part risk: keep "as thick as fingers" and reject any model with snapped tines. The moss tufts belong to the head and must not touch the body. Hind feet larger is fine; hind and front leg **length** must stay within 0.95 to 1.05 (standard 1.1). Rare look (Head texture): antlers birch white, moss tufts and mushrooms Starlight silver.
- **Card:** horned hare of old tales; Arcane; Quad; Common; S, 44; sprinter.
- **Signature parts:** antlers with moss tips (Head); star freckles (Torso); big mitten hind feet (Legs); plus the pompom tail.
- **Personality:** mischievous prankster · gesture **Hop** (a sideways hop and an antler toss). Cry: a quick "tik-tik-hoo". Entrance: bounds in from off-screen and skids to a stop. Journal: "Travellers on the Old Lane blame it for every missing sock." / "Its antlers grow a new moss tip each spring and it rubs off the old one on Lanternmoor's signposts." Favourite: being chased. Rare look: see 3D notes.
