## Alternatives for similar / flagged names

- **Lanternell** (glowing jelly-lamp night-watcher): Glowjell, Lampulet, Bobbelle
- **Carrackon** (hermit wearing a whole ship): Hullhermit, Galleonk, Shipwrackle (avoid "-osta")

- **Cocklemole** (digger in cockle-shell helmet): Shellmole, Scallopmole, Whelkmole
- **Sloughback** (pronunciation only, optional): Sleepnewt, Mudslumber, Sluffback (phonetic spelling)
