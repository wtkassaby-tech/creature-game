# Creature design bible: Stormreach (Storm)

Biome 8 of 11. Town Highwhistle. 5v5. Winged dominates (6 of 10 species), plus two Storm ground species and two Sand underdogs. First sight of the tear. Species from `00_ROSTER_PLAN.md` section 8: Gustlet, Vanecrest, Hailstoop, Boomthroat, Duneswallow, Loomoth, Thunderhump, Puffchin, Kiteling, Gritgoyle. Giant: Squallray.

## 1. Visual identity

**Palette (flat blocks, two main colours plus one accent per creature):**

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main light | Sky white | #F2F6FA |
| Main dark | Slate storm blue | #3B4F6B |
| Main mid | Cloud grey-blue | #8FA3B8 |
| Warm main | Sunlit gold | #F2B94B |
| Accent (signature) | Weathered copper | #B8672F |
| Spark accent (rare look, tiny areas) | Lightning yellow | #FFE45C |
| Sand underdogs | Sandblasted dust | #D9C3A0 |
| Tear-touched | Starfield indigo | #2C2A5E |

Rule: at most one warm colour per creature as a main (gold or copper, not both). Slate and white carry the silhouette; copper is the one-per-creature accent, which makes the biome recognisable on a crowded team screen.

**Materials (painted on clay, per the STYLE LINE):** matte plasticine feathers in chunky layered rows, cotton-wool cloud tufts, copper vanes with a soft satin finish (painted, not reflective), kite cloth as flat stitched panels. Lightning is a painted zigzag stripe, never a glowing or see-through element.

**Shape language:** wedge and swoop. Swept-back crests, long tapering wings, forward-pointing chests, fins and vanes that all lean into the wind, so the whole biome reads as motion. Edges stay rounded and chunky, but every silhouette has a clear diagonal, in contrast to round Greenwood and blocky Emberfall.

**Motifs:** weathervane, wind gauge, kite and tether, cloud-tuft collar, zigzag bolt stripe, feathers layered like roof tiles, hail pellets, chimes. Cap: lightning marks on at most two parts per creature.

## 2. Target mix

Ten species: **4 cool, 3 majestic, 2 cute, 1 creepy-cute**, plus the giant (majestic).

Why:
- The evidence says cool designs outnumber cute ones in the global top 40, and fox, wolf, cat bodies and dragons make up half of the favourites. A sky biome is the natural home for dragon-like and raptor silhouettes, and speed (this biome's identity) reads as cool. So this is the most "cool" biome so far.
- Majestic goes to the big, proud shapes players aspire to own: broad wings, a thundercloud bison.
- Two cute anchors (Gustlet, Puffchin) are XS sprinters, the pocket pets young players bond with. Cute stays in so the biome is not all sharp edges.
- One creepy-cute (Loomoth, the pale moth with a starfield): the tear's first visible touch. Creepy-cute is the most shared and fan-arted look, and one is enough to carry the story beat.
- Suggested split: cool = Hailstoop, Vanecrest, Kiteling, Gritgoyle; majestic = Boomthroat, Thunderhump, Duneswallow; cute = Gustlet, Puffchin; creepy-cute = Loomoth.

## 3. Archetypes that fit (principles only)

- **Dragon-adjacent silhouette without a dragon.** Long swept wings, a crest and a strong chest. At least two Winged species (Hailstoop, Boomthroat) get a broad-shouldered, proud read, so the biome has a poster creature that feels dragon-like and still reads as a bird.
- **One signature feature grown into the body.** The weathervane that is the tail, the cloud that is the hump, the kite that is alive. One big idea from the anatomy beats added accessories. Each species' three signatures sit on three different parts, per the breeding standard.
- **Cat, fox and wolf body language on the ground species.** Thunderhump and Puffchin can carry the loved low, confident, forward-gazing stance while staying a bison and a chinchilla.
- **A personality or story hook.** Vanecrest points at storms, so players say "it knows". Loomoth arrived with the tear. One readable mood per face: alert, proud, sleepy or mischievous.
- **Mascot-ready face.** Big forward eyes on a wedge head, with a clear brow ridge to carry the mood.

## 4. Rare-look (shiny-style) colour rule

Gem tiers are effects, and a rare look is a texture swap on the one part that owns the signature feature (breeding standard 3.5).

**The "Stormlit" rule inverts value, not hue.** The sky-white and slate mains swap (a pale body goes dusk slate, a dark body goes bright white). The copper accent turns **lightning yellow (#FFE45C) on the signature part only**, and the painted bolt stripe turns bright. The species stays instantly recognisable, but one glance says "the dark-sky version, caught in a flash". It stays flat and painted; gem sparkle layers on top and never replaces it.

Why: value contrast reads from across a room (the Roblox rarity cue the evidence points to), it needs no new mesh, and it changes one colour on one part, so it travels with that part when bred.

## 5. The giant: Squallray

**Concept:** a colossal sky manta-ray, wider than Highwhistle, gliding inside the Anvil's storm cloud. Slate-blue back, sky-white underside, copper and yellow zigzag stripes along the wing edges, and a long tapering tail ending in a copper vane. Calm face and slow wingbeats: awe, not fear.

**Fight tell:** unmissable from the silhouette on a phone. In the wind-up the wing-edge zigzags turn solid bright yellow and Squallray tilts both wing tips up for one full beat before it releases the Static drain on the team's Tempo. A Crystal or Arcane hit during the tilt grounds it: the stripes go dull slate for two turns. Fixed effect, no random roll.

## 6. Mascot candidate

**Vanecrest**, the weathervane rooster (already the gem showcase). It has the clearest single signature (a copper vane for a tail), a story hook (it points at storms), a proud readable mood, and the strongest thumbnail silhouette: crest, chest, vane. It is Common, so every player meets it early and bonds with it. It carries the whole palette in one creature: slate body, sky-white chest, copper vane. Runner-up: Boomthroat, whose red throat pouch sits outside the palette.

## 7. Three risks to avoid

1. **Everything reads as "a bird".** Six Winged species can blur into one silhouette. Vary wing shape (short and broad, long and thin, kite, moth), head (crest, pouch, beak) and tail, and test all six as black silhouettes at thumbnail size. No two share head and wing proportions.
2. **Copying a known storm creature.** Avoid electric sheep or squirrels, a yellow mouse, a cloud-bird that reads as an existing storm legendary, and a green sky serpent (`00_ROSTER_PLAN.md` 2b). Lightning is a stripe and a vane, not a mascot rodent. Run the originality check hardest on Hailstoop and Boomthroat, since raptors and thunderbirds are crowded.
3. **Too much lightning, too little colour.** An all-yellow bolt palette or "glowing" electricity breaks the painted-not-glowing rule and flattens the silhouettes. Keep yellow for the rare look and tiny accents, keep copper as the identity accent, and keep wings folding straight down at rest so Winged parts stay breedable across joints.
