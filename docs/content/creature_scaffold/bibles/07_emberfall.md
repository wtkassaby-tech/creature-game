# Creature design bible 07: Emberfall (Fire)

Town Cinderwick, 5v5, heavy hitters, Winged plan debuts. Roster (10): Slagwallow, Smoulderskink, Pyrecone, Tongbeetle, Glassgaffer, Ashbloom, Magmeel, Fusewick, Ashflit, Updrafter. Giant: Cinderhart. Names are placeholders. Sources: 00_ROSTER_PLAN (biome 7), 13_WORLD_DESIGN, the loved/ evidence set. No eras/00_SYNTHESIS.md exists yet.

## 1. Visual identity

**Palette** (two body colours plus one accent per creature, flat blocks):

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body dark | Basalt charcoal | #2E2B30 |
| Body mid | Cooled slate | #5A555C |
| Body light | Ash white | #E4DED6 |
| Accent (the hero) | Ember orange | #FF6A1F |
| Accent hot | Molten amber | #FFB02E |
| Underdog Leaf 1 | Fireweed pink | #D9548C |
| Underdog Leaf 2 | Burnt-cone brown | #7A4A2B |
| Rare-look only | White-hot gold | #FFE9A0 |

Rule: ember orange is always the smallest area on the creature (about 10 to 15 percent), so it reads as the one hot thing. One accent per species. Ash white is the belly, face or bib colour: warm bodies with a cream belly read friendliest in the Fire evidence.

**Materials** (clay-toy, matte, handmade): basalt as chunky matte clay with thumbprint dents; obsidian as the single glossy glaze, only on heads, horns and plates; pumice as speckled lighter clay; cooling slag as cracked crust with seams. Glow is "painted bright, not glowing" (breeding standard): seams are flat orange paint, never see-through. A runtime glow on seams is reserved for rare looks (section 6).

**Shape language:** heavy, blocky, cracked plates; low centre of gravity; thick limbs and big hands and forearms (Attack on front limbs is the biome's breeding stat, so front limbs are the biggest, most expressive part). Plates are rounded-corner slabs, never razor spikes. The two Leaf underdogs are the soft shapes (cone, bud, flower-spike), standing out against the slab bodies.

**Motifs:** glowing seam cracks; stacked basalt columns; tiny ash-white smoke puffs as a mood cue; hammer, tong and anvil (the forge and kiln thread, echoing Kilnrest and Glassgaffer); fireweed and cones (life returning after fire); thermal updraughts (wings fanned upward). Cosiness is the counterweight: Cinderwick is a forge-town, so even the toughest creature carries one homely tell (soot on the nose, a scorched belly patch).

## 2. Target mix across the 10 species

Evidence: cool or majestic is about 68 percent of the top 40, cute about a third (overlapping); fox, wolf, cat and dragon bodies are about half of favourites; Roblox trade lists are topped by dragons, with cute animals strong beside them; Fire is the best-loved type (six of the top 20 favourites). So cool leads, with cute a firm second so younger players have a pet to love.

| Mood | Count | Species | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cool | 4 | Tongbeetle, Magmeel, Ashflit, Fusewick | The biome is about power: forge beetle, obsidian eel, ember bat are readable fighter silhouettes. Fusewick adds a nervous edge. |
| Majestic | 2 | Updrafter, Slagwallow | Large, slow, grand shapes (thermal soarer, magma hippo). Cinderhart the giant is a third. |
| Cute | 3 | Smoulderskink, Pyrecone, Ashbloom | The stocky mascot and the two round Leaf underdogs: the soft contrast. |
| Creepy-cute | 1 | Glassgaffer | An odd glassblower with a tube snout blowing bubbles: weird and endearing. |

That is 60 percent cool or majestic, 40 percent cute or creepy-cute. Sizes are varied on purpose: L bulk (Slagwallow, Tongbeetle) against XS and S sprinters (Ashflit, Fusewick, Ashbloom, Smoulderskink), so the line-up reads as varied, not ten similar grey lumps.

## 3. Archetypes from the loved evidence that fit (principles, not copies)

- **Familiar animal plus one flame feature, heroic stance.** Smoulderskink (lizard with cracked scales) and Slagwallow (hippo with a crust back). Warm base, cream belly.
- **One signature feature grown into the body.** Tongbeetle's tong mandibles and hammer forearms, Glassgaffer's tube snout, Pyrecone's cone body. The feature is the silhouette, on a different part per species.
- **The dragon appetite, answered by the Winged plan.** Dragons top Roblox trade lists. Emberfall has no literal dragon (the Crown and later biomes own the apex), but Updrafter and Ashflit are the wing parts that let players assemble dragon-feeling builds by breeding.
- **One colour trick on a simple body.** Near-black body with one hot accent (the evidence's best fox example is a black body with glowing rings): basalt plus ember seams.
- **A personality hook with one readable mood.** Fusewick nervous and bolting, Ashbloom proud and sunny, Slagwallow lazy-content. One mood per face, legible at thumbnail size.
- **Bulk plus a gentle face.** Heavy things become loved when the expression is soft; Slagwallow and Tongbeetle get friendly eyes.
- **Growth drama.** Fire favourites are starter lines where attachment forms through evolution. Our version is generations: a Smoulderskink line whose cracks widen each generation is a visible, earned story.

## 4. Giant: Cinderhart (Fire, Quad)

**Concept:** a colossal stag. Antlers are smoking basalt columns (stacked slabs with ember seams and a thin ash plume); hooves leave glowing prints. Deep charcoal body, ash-white chest tuft, ember seams down the neck. Majestic first, threatening second: the first sight is a quiet silhouette on Old Smoulder's rim.

**Fight tell:** it Burns the team with every antler sweep. The wind-up is readable on the creature itself: over one turn the antler seams brighten from ember orange to molten amber and the plume thickens. A Water hit during the wind-up quenches the antlers for two turns (seams go dark, plume stops). That is the puzzle, and it rewards the Water answer carried by the Leaf underdogs. The tell is shown, never text alone.

## 5. Mascot candidate: Smoulderskink

A stocky skink is a friendly, familiar animal (lizards are in the top evidence group), deliberately a quad rather than an upright orange lizard to stay clear of the genre's best-known fire starter. It is Common, so every player meets it early, and its silhouette is plain and round with one cracked-scale accent: the "simple readable mascot" lesson from Roblox hits. Cute and cool in one: friendly face, basalt armour, glowing seams. Runner-up: Ashflit, but it is XS and too small to carry a brand.

## 6. Rare-look (shiny-style) colour rule

**The ember seams go white-hot.** The body stays the same. On the one part that owns the feature, the accent shifts from ember orange (#FF6A1F) to white-hot gold (#FFE9A0) with a thin amber rim, and the basalt on that part changes from matte to glossy obsidian. Rarity reads from across a room through brightness, matching the Roblox evidence (glow, outline and size signal rarity). Rare looks may add a gentle runtime glow on those seams and a slow ember drift, never on the common form.

Constraints: the variant belongs to the owning part (breeding standard 3.5) so it travels with that part in breeding; white-hot gold replaces orange rather than adding a fourth colour; on the Leaf underdogs fireweed pink becomes a hot magenta-gold bloom and the cone goes lacquered dark. How rare looks are earned is Ayrton's call, and they come from play, never a paid random outcome.

## 7. Three risks to avoid

1. **Ten grey lumps.** Charcoal everywhere turns to mush at thumbnail size. Give each creature one distinct silhouette feature on a different part, vary size and proportion, and let the Leaf species and Glassgaffer carry their own light colours (pink, brown, glass-blue and orange).
2. **Reading as a known creature.** Fire is the most crowded type. Avoid an upright orange lizard with a flaming tail, a fire horse or dog with a flame mane, a lava snail, a volcano-shell tortoise and a fire moth with sun wings (the roster plan's watch-out list). Flames are cracks and seams, never literal fire plumes. Test each concept with "X in a different colour".
3. **Too dark, too angry.** Volcanic can slide into grim and spiky, against the cosy clay-toy direction and a young audience. Use rounded slabs, soft faces, a warm kiln mood (soot, hammers, bubbles), an ash belly on most species and one friendly tell each. Save menace for Cinderhart's wind-up, and keep even that majestic.

Note for the lead: only Ashflit is a Fire flyer and Updrafter is Storm, so the Winged debut leans on two species; if playtests show it thin, the spare concepts (lava-crust roadrunner, pumice puffball) are the safest growth.
