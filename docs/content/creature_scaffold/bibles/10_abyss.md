# Creature design bible 10: The Abyss (Cosmic)

Date: 23 November 2026. Status: [Default] design guidance; all names are placeholders from `00_ROSTER_PLAN.md`. Town Starfold, 5v5, Diamond rarity begins, last rival battle, then the road to The Hollow. Sources: roster plan section 10, the breeding standard, plan 13 (the Starwell Tunnel, the one place the sky becomes night; the tear over the Rift), and the loved-creature research (legendaries, Roblox hits, toys and plush).

## 1. Visual identity

**Mood in one line:** a quiet planetarium made of clay. Dark, calm and slow, but warm to the touch. The Abyss must never read as horror; it is the night the whole game has been walking toward.

**Palette (2 to 3 body colours plus one accent per creature, drawn from this set)**

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base dark | Midnight navy | #14183A |
| Base dark | Velvet black | #0B0B14 |
| Base mid | Dusk indigo | #2E2A66 |
| Base mid | Smoked glass grey | #4A4E69 |
| Light | Starlight white | #F4F1E8 |
| Accent | Nebula pink | #E56FA8 |
| Accent | Nebula teal | #38C7C0 |
| Accent | Gold thread | #E8B94A |

Rule: one dark base plus one mid or light colour, then a single accent from pink, teal or gold. Gold thread is the biome signature and is used as linework only, never as a large fill.

**Materials.** Matte plasticine stays the base (the Style Line is fixed). Cosmic touches are painted or pressed into the clay, not shiny: speckled star dots, fine gold thread lines joining star points like stitched constellations (the game's patchwork theme at its most cosmic), and one small smoked-glass or translucent resin element per creature (an eye, a shell window, a ring). No metallic chrome, no gradient space photography.

**Shape language.** Sleek, ribbon-like, ringed and domed; long soft curves and orbits, with chunky, rounded proportions kept at the joints so the breeding standard's ball ends and bands still fit. Slow and floating: relaxed poses, with tails and streamers as the long gestures. Circles and arcs over spikes.

**Motifs.** Constellation lines, rings and orbits, crescents, hourglass and telescope forms, comet trails, a doorway or window onto another sky. One motif per creature.

**Part rule (breeding).** Put the signature on the one part that owns it (Cometkit's tail, Orrerback's torso shell). Breeding stat is Speed on tails, so tails are the biome's expressive, swappable part and should read well on any torso. Gold thread and star speckle live in the texture of each part so they travel with it.

## 2. Target mix across the 10 species

| Feel | Target | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cool | 4 | The research shows cool outnumbers cute in the global top 40, and dark, night-themed dragons top Roblox trade demand. This is the endgame, where players want status. |
| Majestic | 3 | Legendaries rank when they read as a character with a one-sentence cosmic concept. Orrerback (Diamond showcase), Nebulark and Ouroring carry scale and awe. |
| Cute | 2 | Cometkit and Spyglint keep the biome approachable and give breeders a friendly part to mix. Cute wins plush appeal. |
| Creepy-cute | 1 | Portaloop (a mouth opening onto another starfield) gives the one uncanny note, softened by clay and big eyes. |

Suggested slotting: Cool: Bolide, Galaxeel, Cometfinch, Hourling. Majestic: Orrerback, Nebulark, Ouroring. Cute: Cometkit, Spyglint. Creepy-cute: Portaloop. Five of ten are Winged or Serpent plans, which gives the biome its floating, sleek silhouette set.

## 3. Archetypes from the loved evidence that fit

Principles only, no copies of any creature.
- **One signature feature grown into the body.** The star-chart shell, the comet tail, the telescope eyes, the hourglass torso. Each species has exactly one.
- **A one-sentence cosmic concept.** "A tortoise carrying a star chart." If it needs a paragraph, cut it.
- **Fox, wolf, cat bodies and dragons make up half the favourites.** Quad and Serpent plans here lean sleek: a fox-lean silhouette for Cometkit, a dragon-like long line for Galaxeel and Ouroring. Serpents are the Abyss's dragon stand-in.
- **One readable mood per creature.** Calm, curious, sleepy or proud; never two at once.
- **A personality or story hook.** Tie the quiet creatures to the sky: Nebulark sings in near silence, Spyglint watches the tear, Portaloop is always looking somewhere else.
- **Night and dark themes sell.** The dark-and-glow palette matches what trades highest on Roblox.
- **Legendary restraint.** Too many legendaries kill scarcity, so keep the grandest silhouettes (rings, giant crest) to few species.

## 4. Rare-look (shiny-style) rule

Rarity must be visible on Roblox through glow, size or outline. The Abyss rare look is the **Eclipse look**: the dark base inverts to starlight white (#F4F1E8) with a soft indigo (#2E2A66) outline, and the accent becomes full-strength gold (#E8B94A) with a faint glow on the gold thread and star dots. Rules:
1. It is a texture variant on the one part that owns the signature feature, so it travels in breeding (breeding standard 3.5), never a plain tint.
2. Only the gold thread, star dots and the glass element glow; the clay stays matte.
3. The silhouette is unchanged, so players learn the species first and the rarity second.
4. Diamond tier uses the same Eclipse treatment with a thicker gold outline, so Diamond reads at a glance beside Ruby.

## 5. The giant: Starfathom

**Concept.** A colossal void oarfish rising from the Starwell: a long clay ribbon of velvet black, speckled with stars, crowned with a crest of pale starlight (#F4F1E8) and one gold thread running its full length like a constellation line. Calm and enormous rather than angry; it is the sky's own creature.

**Fight tell.** It Drifts the team (no switching for two turns, a fixed effect with no random rolls) while it charges. The tell is readable: its crest lifts and brightens turn by turn (dim, half, full) while the gold thread draws slowly toward the head. A Crystal or Storm hit during the wind-up breaks the charge, and the crest visibly snaps dark. Players learn "watch the crest."

## 6. Mascot candidate: Orrerback

The Diamond showcase and the roster's star. Why: it has a one-sentence concept, a signature feature grown into the body and a calm readable mood, which is the winning pattern in the evidence; its tortoise body is friendly and plush-like; the star-chart shell with gold thread is the biome's whole look in a single image; and as the quest gift it is the creature most players will meet. Fallback: Cometkit, as the cute entry point.

## 7. Three risks to avoid

1. **Too dark to read.** Midnight navy against a night sky loses the silhouette on phones. Keep a light edge (starlight white or an accent outline) on every creature, and test each at phone size against the Abyss night preset.
2. **Cosmic noise.** Too many stars, rings and trails at once break the one-feature rule and the part separation the breeding standard needs. One motif per creature, with speckle kept sparse and inside single parts.
3. **Losing the clay-toy feel.** Gradients, chrome and glow-heavy space art would clash with every other biome. Everything stays matte plasticine with painted detail; glow is reserved for the Eclipse rare look and the smallest accents. Keep Portaloop friendly: big eyes, soft mouth, never menacing.
